Day Trips · Campground

Myakka River State Park Campgrounds and Cabins

Note Open. The park's drinking water has been unavailable since Oct. 28, 2025; bring water (free bottled water at the ranger station). Cabins require two nights on weekends and holidays.

Three campgrounds (Big Flats, Old Prairie and Palmetto Ridge) with 90 sites, each with electric, water, a fire ring and a picnic table (Palmetto Ridge also has sewer), plus six hike-in primitive campgrounds of three sites each and five historic palm-log cabins built by the Civilian Conservation Corps between 1934 and 1941, each with a bathroom, a kitchen and room for six. Pets are allowed in the developed campgrounds but not in cabins or primitive sites.
