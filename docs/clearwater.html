Sheet 4 · CW

Clearwater & North Pinellas

Pinellas County's mainland north of St. Petersburg: the City of Clearwater's waterfront downtown and aquarium, and small towns up the coast and around Old Tampa Bay. Scottish-influenced Dunedin has breweries and the gateway to Honeymoon Island State Park, where a ferry leaves for Caladesi Island; Safety Harbor has mineral springs and a historic resort, Tarpon Springs has its Greek sponge docks, and Palm Harbor's Innisbrook hosts the Valspar Championship.

Known for Clearwater Marine Aquarium, Dunedin breweries, Honeymoon and Caladesi islands, the Tarpon Springs Sponge Docks, Safety Harbor Resort & Spa and Phillies and Blue Jays spring training.

CWDowntown Clearwater

Clearwater · City

Downtown Clearwater

The mainland City of Clearwater, across the Clearwater Memorial Causeway from the beach, with a walkable downtown, Coachman Park and its BayCare Sound amphitheater, the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre and the Clearwater Marine Aquarium, which cares for rescued dolphins and sea turtles. The Philadelphia Phillies hold spring training here, and Ruth Eckerd Hall and the Countryside area are included in this part of the guide.

Clearwater Marine Aquarium · Coachman Park and The BayCare Sound · the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre · Ruth Eckerd Hall · Philadelphia Phillies spring training

CWDunedin

Clearwater · City

Dunedin

A small, Scottish-influenced city just north of Clearwater with a walkable, bike-friendly downtown of independent boutiques, cafes and breweries on the Pinellas Trail. The Dunedin Causeway leads to Honeymoon Island State Park, where a ferry leaves for Caladesi Island State Park, and the Toronto Blue Jays train each spring at TD Ballpark.

downtown breweries, including Dunedin Brewery · Honeymoon Island State Park · Caladesi Island State Park · Scottish heritage · Toronto Blue Jays spring training at TD Ballpark · the Pinellas Trail

16 places · 8 places to stay · 3 experiences · 21 events

CWSafety Harbor

Clearwater · City

Safety Harbor

A small city on Old Tampa Bay with brick streets under moss-draped oaks, a Main Street of restaurants and shops, and the historic Safety Harbor Resort & Spa, established in 1925 on natural mineral springs once believed to be the Fountain of Youth. Philippe Park, the oldest park in Pinellas County, holds the Tocobaga Temple Mound, the largest remaining mound in the Tampa Bay region.

the Safety Harbor Resort & Spa · its natural springs · Main Street restaurants and craft beer · the Tocobaga Temple Mound at Philippe Park · the Safety Harbor Museum & Cultural Center

CWTarpon Springs

Clearwater · City

Tarpon Springs

A Gulf coast city about 30 minutes north of Clearwater whose sponge industry, worked by more than 500 Greek sponge divers by 1905, built the Greek community it is known for. The Sponge Docks along pedestrian-friendly Dodecanese Boulevard still draw visitors to sponge boats, shops and Greek restaurants; Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says the city has the largest Greek community per capita in the United States.

the Sponge Docks · Greek restaurants on Dodecanese Boulevard · natural sea sponges · Fred Howard Park · Anclote Key Preserve State Park

CWPalm Harbor & Innisbrook

Clearwater · County area

Palm Harbor & Innisbrook

An unincorporated community about 20 minutes north of Clearwater, with a small downtown of shops, eateries and historic buildings. Innisbrook Golf Resort's Copperhead course hosts the PGA Tour's annual Valspar Championship, and John Chesnut Sr. Park in the East Lake area and Wall Springs Park are its best-known parks.

Innisbrook Golf Resort · the Valspar Championship on the Copperhead course · downtown Palm Harbor and the Palm Harbor Museum · John Chesnut Sr. Park · Wall Springs Park

CWOldsmar

Clearwater · City

Oldsmar

A small city at the top of Old Tampa Bay that Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says has more parks per square mile than any other city in Florida, including Mobbly Bayou Wilderness Preserve and R.E. Olds Park. The Tampa Bay Downs thoroughbred racetrack is in Oldsmar, as is the Oldsmar Flea Market.

Tampa Bay Downs · Mobbly Bayou Wilderness Preserve · R.E. Olds Park · the Oldsmar Flea Market

CWLargo & Mid-Pinellas

Clearwater · City

Largo & Mid-Pinellas

Largo is a largely residential city with a natural side: the Florida Botanical Gardens and Heritage Village, Largo Central Park with the Largo Central Railroad, and parks, trails and public golf courses. This area also covers Seminole, a gateway to Madeira Beach and Indian Shores with Lake Seminole Park; Pinellas Park, with the Tampa Bay Automobile Museum and nearby Sawgrass Lake Park; and Belleair and Kenneth City.

Florida Botanical Gardens · Heritage Village · Largo Central Park · Lake Seminole Park · Sawgrass Lake Park · the Pinellas Trail

CWClearwater Marine Aquarium

Clearwater · Aquarium · Downtown Clearwater

Clearwater Marine Aquarium

Nonprofit aquarium and working marine animal hospital that rescues, rehabilitates and releases marine life; visitors see resident dolphins, sea turtles, and California sea lions and harbor seals at the new Pinniped Pier. It keeps a 'Tales of Winter the Dolphin' exhibit.

3
CWHoneymoon Island State Park

Clearwater · State park · Dunedin

Honeymoon Island State Park

A barrier-island state park just west of Dunedin with about four miles of Gulf beach (swimming on the main beach, a pet beach on the south end, shelling on the northern sand spit), the 2.5-mile Osprey Trail through virgin slash pine, the Rotary Centennial Nature Center, a cafe and rentals of bikes, kayaks, umbrellas and chairs. The ferry to Caladesi Island leaves from here.

4
CWCaladesi Island State Park

Clearwater · State park · Dunedin

Caladesi Island State Park

An undeveloped barrier island reached by ferry from Honeymoon Island (the operator calls it a 20-minute ride) or by private boat, with three miles of beach, a three-mile nature trail, a three-mile kayak trail through the mangroves, a marina with electric and water hookups, picnic pavilions, a snack bar and a gift shop. The ferry operator says Dr. Beach voted it the No. 3 beach of 2026.

7
CWRuth Eckerd Hall

Clearwater · Performing arts · Downtown Clearwater

Ruth Eckerd Hall

Performing arts hall on McMullen Booth Road in Clearwater, opened in 1983, with the smaller Murray Theatre and a cabaret theater on the same campus. Ruth Eckerd Hall also runs the Capitol Theatre and The BayCare Sound downtown and the Marcia P. Hoffman School of the Arts.

1
CWTarpon Springs Sponge Docks

Clearwater · District · Tarpon Springs

Tarpon Springs Sponge Docks

Historic waterfront along Dodecanese Boulevard, center of Tarpon Springs' Greek sponge industry: docked sponge boats, sponge and souvenir shops, Greek restaurants and bakeries, sponge-diving exhibits, and sightseeing and dolphin cruises on the Anclote River.

2
CWHellas Restaurant & Bakery

Clearwater · Restaurant · Tarpon Springs

Hellas Restaurant & Bakery

Greek restaurant and pastry bakery on the Sponge Docks, run by the Karterouliotis family since 1970; the menu runs from gyros and Greek salads to lamb chops and local seafood, and the bakery sells Greek pastries to eat in or take away.

5
CWDunedin Brewery

Clearwater · Brewery · Dunedin

Dunedin Brewery

Brewpub in downtown Dunedin brewing since 1996, with pub food and live music on two stages (the Pub Stage and the Moon Tower). The brewery calls itself Florida's oldest craft brewery, which it defines as the oldest distributing microbrewery still in operation.

CWFred Howard Park

Clearwater · Beach · Tarpon Springs

Fred Howard Park

A 155-acre county park in Tarpon Springs where a 1-mile causeway leads to a Gulf beach used for swimming, windsurfing, kayak launching and sunsets; the mainland side has picnic shelters, playgrounds and a ball field.

Daily, 7 a.m. to sunset; closed the day after Thanksgiving and Dec. 25. · $6 daily beach parking.

CWHoneymoon Island State Park

Clearwater · State park · Dunedin

Honeymoon Island State Park

A barrier-island state park just west of Dunedin with about four miles of Gulf beach (swimming on the main beach, a pet beach on the south end, shelling on the northern sand spit), the 2.5-mile Osprey Trail through virgin slash pine, the Rotary Centennial Nature Center, a cafe and rentals of bikes, kayaks, umbrellas and chairs. The ferry to Caladesi Island leaves from here.

Daily, 8 a.m. until sundown, 365 days a year. Rotary Centennial Nature Center: Wednesday to Sunday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. · $8 per vehicle (up to eight people); $4 single-occupant vehicle; $2 pedestrians, bicyclists and extra passengers; $4 per vehicle sunset fee in the last hour before closing.

CWCaladesi Island State Park

Clearwater · State park · Dunedin

Caladesi Island State Park

An undeveloped barrier island reached by ferry from Honeymoon Island (the operator calls it a 20-minute ride) or by private boat, with three miles of beach, a three-mile nature trail, a three-mile kayak trail through the mangroves, a marina with electric and water hookups, picnic pavilions, a snack bar and a gift shop. CWAnclote Key Preserve State Park

Clearwater · State park · Tarpon Springs

Anclote Key Preserve State Park

An 11,773-acre island state park three miles off Tarpon Springs, reached only by private boat or by ferries from the Sponge Docks; its four islands (Anclote Key, North Anclote Bar, South Anclote Bar and Three Rooker Island) have beaches, shorebird nesting areas and an 1887 lighthouse, open only during special events. Primitive camping is allowed on the north end of Anclote Key, but ferries do not carry overnight campers; there is no water, only two compostable toilets.

Daily, 8 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. · No park fee.

CWPhilippe Park

Clearwater · Park · Safety Harbor

Philippe Park

Pinellas County's oldest park, 92 acres in Safety Harbor, built around the Tocobaga temple mound of the Safety Harbor Site, a National Historic Landmark.

Daily, 7 a.m. to sunset; closed the day after Thanksgiving and Dec. 25. · Boat ramp fee charged.

CWLargo Central Park

Largo Central Park

City of Largo park on Central Park Drive that hosts city events such as the free Largo Vibe Fest arts festival (Oct 2-4, 2026) and the Holiday Lights walk (Nov 26, 2026 - Jan 3, 2027), a display of more than 2.5 million LED lights.

Sheet 4 · 13 ways to get here and around

Getting around

Getting around

Arriving

  • St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport PIEAirportThe airport on Roosevelt Blvd (SR 686), 4 miles north of St. Petersburg, served by Allegiant and Sun Country (domestic) and BermudAir (international), with nonstops to more than 60 cities. Rideshare pickup is in front of the terminal at Arrivals; taxis and shuttles wait in the Ground Transportation lot outside baggage claim; PSTA's Grouper runs to Clearwater Beach.

Transit, trolleys and boats

  • Clearwater FerryWater taxiA passenger ferry linking downtown Clearwater (Coachman Park), the Clearwater Beach Marina near Pier 60 and the Dunedin Marina, so you can reach the beach without driving and parking. Reservations are recommended but not required; walk-ons board if there is room, and service can be suspended in bad weather.
  • PSTA Route 300 Express (Largo to Tampa Airport and downtown Tampa)Bus · Regular PSTA fare ($2.25).A weekday limited-stop express from the Ulmerton Park and Ride in Largo via the Tampa International Airport Rental Car Center to the Marion Transit Center in downtown Tampa.
  • PSTA busesBusPinellas County's transit agency: fixed-route buses, the SunRunner rapid bus, the Suncoast Beach Trolley along the Gulf beaches, and express routes 100 and 300 to downtown Tampa. Tap a contactless card (not American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay or the Flamingo Fares app to get the daily and monthly fare caps.
  • Jolley TrolleyTrolleyTrolleys on Clearwater Beach (North Beach and South Beach routes around the beach, Island Estates and Sand Key, as far south as Bay Park) and a Coastal Route from Clearwater Beach and downtown Clearwater to Dunedin, Palm Harbor and the Tarpon Springs Sponge Docks. The fare is $2.25; the trolleys carry two bikes on a front rack and do not go to John's Pass.
  • Suncoast Beach TrolleyTrolleyA PSTA trolley route along Gulf Boulevard linking the Gulf beach towns from St. Pete Beach (75th Ave) through Treasure Island, John's Pass, Madeira Beach, the Redingtons, Indian Shores, Indian Rocks Beach and Sand Key to Clearwater Beach and the Park Street Terminal in downtown Clearwater.
  • Grouper Airport Express (PIE to Clearwater Beach)MicrotransitA shared, bookable shuttle between St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport (PIE) and any address on Clearwater Beach, including the Sheraton and Marriott Sand Key resorts; book through grouper.psta.net or the Grouper app. At PIE the pickup is through the west baggage claim exit doors; drivers help with luggage and the service is wheelchair accessible.
