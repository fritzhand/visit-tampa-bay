Sheet 3 · GB

Gulf Beaches

A chain of barrier islands along Pinellas County's Gulf coast, from Clearwater Beach south through Sand Key, Indian Rocks Beach, Madeira Beach, Treasure Island and St. Pete Beach to Pass-a-Grille and Fort De Soto. Each beach town has its own character, from Clearwater Beach's resorts and Pier 60 to Pass-a-Grille's low-rise historic district and Fort De Soto's county park beaches.

Known for white-sand Gulf beaches, Pier 60 sunsets, John's Pass Village & Boardwalk, the Don CeSar, Fort De Soto Park and dolphin and shelling tours.

Areas and towns on this sheet

All areas

Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Clearwater Beach

The City of Clearwater's beach, on a barrier island joined to the mainland by the Clearwater Memorial Causeway, with about 2.5 miles of white quartz sand and calm, shallow water just steps from hotels and restaurants. Pier 60 anchors the beach and hosts the nightly Sunsets at Pier 60 celebration with street performers and vendors.

white quartz sand · Pier 60 and Sunsets at Pier 60 · pirate, dolphin and dinner cruises · parasailing and jet skis · beachfront resorts

16 places · 35 places to stay · 18 experiences · 4 events

GBSand Key & Belleair Beach

Gulf Beaches · Island

Sand Key & Belleair Beach

Sand Key is the barrier island that runs from Clearwater south to Madeira Beach; this area covers its northern end, joined to Clearwater Beach by a short bridge. The 95-acre Sand Key Park in Clearwater has a white sand beach with seasonal lifeguards and sea turtle nesting from April to October, and the small cities of Belleair Beach and Belleair Shore lie to its south.

Sand Key Park · sea turtle nesting · salt marsh views

2 places · 3 places to stay · 1 event

GBIndian Rocks Beach & Indian Shores

Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Indian Rocks Beach & Indian Shores

A long, quieter stretch of beach towns between Clearwater Beach and Madeira Beach, where mom-and-pop shops, small motels and vacation rentals line three miles of gently sloping beach in Indian Rocks Beach, and the long shoreline suits walks and shelling. Indian Shores has the Seaside Seabird Sanctuary, and this area also covers North Redington Beach and Redington Shores.

quiet beaches for shelling · small motels and vacation rentals · the Seaside Seabird Sanctuary · the Indian Rocks Historical Museum · beachside restaurants and bars

13 places · 17 places to stay

GBMadeira Beach & John's Pass

Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Madeira Beach & John's Pass

A barrier island town that Visit St. Pete/Clearwater likens to an Old Florida fishing village crossed with a beach town, with about two miles of beach. John's Pass Village & Boardwalk, on the waterfront at John's Pass, has more than 100 merchants, including shops and restaurants, the local fishing fleet, and dolphin, shelling, parasail and jet ski outfitters; Redington Beach, a small residential town, is included here.

John's Pass Village & Boardwalk · fishing charters and the local fishing fleet · dolphin and shelling tours · Hubbard's Marina

7 places · 7 places to stay · 9 experiences · 2 events

GBTreasure Island

Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Treasure Island

A barrier island city between St. Pete Beach and Madeira Beach with nearly four miles of extra-wide white-sand beach in three main areas: Sunshine Beach, Municipal Beach and Sunset Beach. Visit St. Pete/Clearwater describes it as a more residential, laid-back stretch of coast; a beach trail runs along the sand, and an international sand sculpting festival is held every fall.

extra-wide white-sand beaches · Sunset Beach · the Treasure Island Beach Trail · a fall sand sculpting festival · ADA beach mats

6 places · 35 places to stay · 1 experience · 1 event

GBSt. Pete Beach

Gulf Beaches · Beach town

St. Pete Beach

A beach city on seven miles of Gulf shoreline just west of downtown St. Petersburg, with wide sandy beaches, warm shallow water, beachfront resorts and the landmark Don CeSar hotel. Its beach bar scene gathers around Corey Avenue, and the historic Pass-a-Grille district is at its southern tip.

the Don CeSar · Upham Beach Park · Corey Avenue · beachfront resorts · water sports outfitters

7 places · 31 places to stay · 4 experiences · 5 events

GBPass-a-Grille

Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Pass-a-Grille

The historic southern tip of St. Pete Beach and one of the area's quietest beach communities, where Visit St. Pete/Clearwater notes there is almost no building directly on the sand. 8th Avenue has pastel-colored shops, galleries and restaurants, the Merry Pier is a historic fishing spot, and a shuttle runs to the undeveloped Shell Key Preserve.

sunsets on the beach · 8th Avenue shops and galleries · the Merry Pier · the Shell Key Shuttle

6 places · 7 places to stay · 1 experience

GBFort De Soto & Tierra Verde

Gulf Beaches · Island

Fort De Soto & Tierra Verde

Fort De Soto, the largest park in the Pinellas County park system, covers 1,136 acres on five connected islands, with beaches, a 236-site campground, a dog beach, a 6.8-mile paved trail and the fort's 12-inch mortar battery, listed on the National Register in 1977. Ferries run from the park to Egmont Key and Shell Key, and Tierra Verde, the boating community on the way in, has marinas, fishing spots and casual restaurants.

Fort De Soto Park beaches · the historic fort and mortar battery · camping · ferries to Egmont Key and Shell Key · Tierra Verde marinas

GBClearwater Beach (public beach)

Gulf Beaches · Beach · Clearwater Beach

Clearwater Beach (public beach)

The City of Clearwater's public Gulf beach at the west end of State Road 60, with public entrances from South Gulfview Boulevard near Opal Sands to Eldorado Avenue, lifeguards year round (towers staffed daily 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.), volleyball, and the Beach Walk promenade. No alcohol, no animals except service animals, and no fishing on the public bathing beaches.

2
GBPier 60

Gulf Beaches · Pier · Clearwater Beach

Pier 60

A 1,080-foot fishing pier with a bait house (bait, tackle, rentals, snacks), next to a beachfront park with a large beach playground, covered pavilions and telescopes. Sunsets at Pier 60, a free nightly festival of artisans and street performers held since 1995, takes place in the park.

6
GBFort De Soto Park

Gulf Beaches · Park · Fort De Soto & Tierra Verde

Fort De Soto Park

Pinellas County's largest park: 1,136 acres on five connected keys with almost three miles of beach (including the only county-park beach where dogs are allowed, in a designated area), two fishing piers, a 236-site campground, seven miles of paved trail, kayak and canoe trails, and the fort's historic mortar battery. Ferries leave from the park for Egmont Key and Shell Key.

4
GBJohn's Pass Village & Boardwalk

Gulf Beaches · District · Madeira Beach & John's Pass

John's Pass Village & Boardwalk

Waterfront boardwalk village at John's Pass with more than 100 merchants: shops, restaurants, the local fishing fleet, dolphin-watching and shelling tours, boat rentals, parasailing and jet skis. The beach is a short walk away.

3
GBFrenchy's Rockaway Grill

Gulf Beaches · Restaurant · Clearwater Beach

Frenchy's Rockaway Grill

Frenchy's largest restaurant, open-air on the sand at Clearwater Beach, serving Gulf seafood including the grouper sandwich and she-crab soup, with two bars and live music daily from local artists. The Frenchy's group says it has been a local favorite since 1981.

5
GBThe Hurricane Seafood Restaurant

Gulf Beaches · Restaurant · Pass-a-Grille

The Hurricane Seafood Restaurant

Seafood restaurant on Gulf Way in Pass-a-Grille, opened in 1977 by the Falkenstein family on the site of the old Casa Bonita Hotel; its Hurricane Watch rooftop deck has twin bars and 360-degree views.

GBSand Key Park

Gulf Beaches · Beach · Sand Key & Belleair Beach

Sand Key Park

A 95-acre county beach park on Sand Key in Clearwater, with a Gulf beach with seasonal lifeguards, a boardwalk nature trail by a salt marsh, bathhouses, a dog park, a playground and umbrella and cabana rentals.

Daily, 7 a.m. to sunset; closed the day after Thanksgiving and Dec. 25. · $6 daily beach parking (credit cards only at pay stations).

GBTreasure Island Beach

Gulf Beaches · Beach · Treasure Island

Treasure Island Beach

About three miles of city beach, wide in most parts, with the .85-mile Treasure Island Beach Trail mid-island (between 104th and 119th avenues) for walking and biking, restrooms and showers at Gulf Front Park and the Sunset Beach Pavilion, and an ADA beach mat at Gulf Front Park. The city's beach signs say no dogs, smoking or vaping, glass, motorized vehicles or e-bikes on the beach.

Hours not listed · City lots $5.00 an hour weekdays, $5.50 weekends (pay by text or ParkMobile).

Getting around

Getting around

Arriving

  • St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport PIEAirportThe airport on Roosevelt Blvd (SR 686), 4 miles north of St. Petersburg, served by Allegiant and Sun Country (domestic) and BermudAir (international), with nonstops to more than 60 cities. Rideshare pickup is in front of the terminal at Arrivals; taxis and shuttles wait in the Ground Transportation lot outside baggage claim; PSTA's Grouper runs to Clearwater Beach.

Transit, trolleys and boats

  • Clearwater FerryWater taxiA passenger ferry linking downtown Clearwater (Coachman Park), the Clearwater Beach Marina near Pier 60 and the Dunedin Marina, so you can reach the beach without driving and parking. Reservations are recommended but not required; walk-ons board if there is room, and service can be suspended in bad weather.
  • PSTA busesBusPinellas County's transit agency: fixed-route buses, the SunRunner rapid bus, the Suncoast Beach Trolley along the Gulf beaches, and express routes 100 and 300 to downtown Tampa. Tap a contactless card (not American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay or the Flamingo Fares app to get the daily and monthly fare caps.
  • SunRunnerBus rapid transitTampa Bay's first bus rapid transit line, a 10-mile, 16-stop route with dedicated bus lanes between downtown St. Petersburg and St. Pete Beach via the Grand Central and EDGE districts and South Pasadena; PSTA says the trip takes about 35 minutes. Buses have level boarding, three interior bike racks, free Wi-Fi and charging at every seat.
  • Jolley TrolleyTrolleyTrolleys on Clearwater Beach (North Beach and South Beach routes around the beach, Island Estates and Sand Key, as far south as Bay Park) and a Coastal Route from Clearwater Beach and downtown Clearwater to Dunedin, Palm Harbor and the Tarpon Springs Sponge Docks. The fare is $2.25; the trolleys carry two bikes on a front rack and do not go to John's Pass.
  • Suncoast Beach TrolleyTrolleyA PSTA trolley route along Gulf Boulevard linking the Gulf beach towns from St. Pete Beach (75th Ave) through Treasure Island, John's Pass, Madeira Beach, the Redingtons, Indian Shores, Indian Rocks Beach and Sand Key to Clearwater Beach and the Park Street Terminal in downtown Clearwater.
  • Free Beach RideMicrotransit · Free; the operator says its drivers welcome tips.An on-demand, door-to-door shuttle on the southern Gulf beaches, from the south tip of Pass-a-Grille to the northern parts of Madeira Beach. Call 727-776-7553, request a ride online or use its app when you are ready to go; it takes no advance bookings and does not go to the airports.
  • Grouper Airport Express (PIE to Clearwater Beach)MicrotransitA shared, bookable shuttle between St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport (PIE) and any address on Clearwater Beach, including the Sheraton and Marriott Sand Key resorts; book through grouper.psta.net or the Grouper app. At PIE the pickup is through the west baggage claim exit doors; drivers help with luggage and the service is wheelchair accessible.
