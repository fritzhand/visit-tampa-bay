Sheet 5 · AB

Around the Bay

The suburbs and small cities around Tampa in Hillsborough and Pasco counties: Temple Terrace and Plant City, Hillsborough's other incorporated cities; unincorporated Brandon, Riverview, Apollo Beach and Ruskin; and Pasco County's Wesley Chapel, Zephyrhills, Dade City and New Port Richey. Visitors come for the Florida Strawberry Festival, winter manatee viewing at Apollo Beach, spring and river parks, outlet and mall shopping, and Pasco's small Gulf coast towns.

Known for the Florida Strawberry Festival, the Manatee Viewing Center, Lithia Springs and Alafia River parks, Tampa Premium Outlets and The Shops at Wiregrass, Dade City antiques and Skydive City.

ABTemple Terrace

Around the Bay · City

Temple Terrace

A small city next to Tampa in northeastern Hillsborough County, founded in 1920 as one of the first planned golf-course communities in the United States, with Mediterranean Revival architecture, and named for the Temple orange. The Hillsborough River runs through the city, and Florida College occupies some of its historic buildings.

1920s Mediterranean Revival architecture · its historic golf course · the Hillsborough River and Riverhills Drive · Florida College

1 place · 8 places to stay

Around the Bay · County area

Brandon & Riverview

A large unincorporated suburb about 11 miles east of downtown Tampa, with the Brandon Exchange mall and its more than 140 stores. The area also takes in Lithia Springs Park, where a spring that stays at 72 degrees allows swimming all year, and Alafia River State Park, with 17 miles of mountain bike trails. Includes Riverview, Valrico and Seffner.

Brandon Exchange mall · Lithia Springs Park · Alafia River State Park mountain biking

5 places · 26 places to stay · 1 event

Around the Bay · County area

Apollo Beach & Ruskin

Unincorporated waterfront communities on Tampa Bay south of Tampa. Apollo Beach has about 55 miles of canals and Tampa Electric's Manatee Viewing Center, where manatees gather in winter in the warm discharge canal of the Big Bend Power Station; the center is open daily from November 1 to April 15. E.G. Simmons Regional Park, north of Ruskin, has a bay beach, a mangrove bird sanctuary, a fishing pier and kayak rentals. Includes Ruskin, Gibsonton and Sun City Center.

the Manatee Viewing Center · E.G. Simmons Regional Park · boating and fishing canals · the Big Bend Power Station

ABPlant City

Around the Bay · City

Plant City

A small city between Tampa and Orlando, founded in 1885 as the railroad arrived and named for Henry B. Plant, known for its strawberry industry. The Florida Strawberry Festival is held here each spring, and the historic downtown hosts events such as Oktoberfest, Last Friday and the Strawberry Drop. Includes Dover and Thonotosassa, home of Hillsborough River State Park.

strawberries · the Florida Strawberry Festival · historic downtown · Dinosaur World · Hillsborough River State Park

ABWesley Chapel & East Pasco

Around the Bay · County area

Wesley Chapel & East Pasco

South and east Pasco County, north of Tampa. Wesley Chapel has The Shops at Wiregrass, Tampa Premium Outlets, the Epperson Lagoon swimming complex and Saddlebrook Resort; Zephyrhills is known for Skydive City and its World War II airfield museum, and Dade City for a historic downtown around its courthouse and for antique shopping. Includes Lutz, Land O' Lakes and Odessa.

The Shops at Wiregrass · Tampa Premium Outlets · Saddlebrook Resort · Skydive City in Zephyrhills · Dade City's historic downtown and antiques · the Pioneer Florida Museum

ABNew Port Richey & West Pasco

Around the Bay · County area

New Port Richey & West Pasco

Pasco County's Gulf coast, centered on New Port Richey, whose walkable downtown on the Cotee River has Sims Park, the restored Hacienda hotel where Hollywood stars once stayed, and the 1925 Richey Suncoast Theatre. Kayak rentals run through the river's mangrove tunnels. Includes Port Richey, Holiday and Hudson.

The Hacienda · Sims Park · kayaking the Cotee River · the Richey Suncoast Theatre

ABFlorida Strawberry Festival Grounds

Around the Bay · Attraction · Plant City

Florida Strawberry Festival Grounds

Festival grounds in Plant City that host the Florida Strawberry Festival, an 11-day fair each spring with headline concerts, livestock shows, agricultural and craft exhibits and strawberry shortcake. The 2027 festival runs March 4-14.

1
ABRobert J. Strickland Memorial Park

Around the Bay · Beach · New Port Richey & West Pasco

Robert J. Strickland Memorial Park

A 6-acre Pasco County beach park in Hudson with a beach and swim area, a boardwalk, an observation tower, a dock, kayak and canoe access, picnic shelters and restaurants nearby; the boat ramp is at 14104 Armand Way.

2
ABHistoric Downtown Dade City

Around the Bay · Historic district · Wesley Chapel & East Pasco

Historic Downtown Dade City

The downtown of Pasco County's seat, about 40 miles north of Tampa, with antique stores, boutiques and independent restaurants around the 1909 Pasco County Courthouse. A self-guided walking tour covers the town's historic buildings, and the Dade City Kumquat Festival fills downtown each January.

6
ABRobert W. Willaford Railroad Museum

Around the Bay · Museum · Plant City

Robert W. Willaford Railroad Museum

A free railroad museum in historic downtown Plant City, in the 1909 Union Depot, with railroad artifacts, vintage trains and a caboose, and a train viewing platform where you can watch trains pass.

3
ABDowntown New Port Richey

Around the Bay · District · New Port Richey & West Pasco

Downtown New Port Richey

A walkable riverfront downtown on the Pithlachascotee (Cotee) River with 1920s Mediterranean Revival buildings, including the Hacienda Hotel and the 1925 Richey Suncoast Theatre. Sims Park, donated in 1913, has walking paths, playgrounds, river views and a kayak launch, and downtown has restaurants and a rooftop bar.

4
ABKojak's House of Ribs

Around the Bay · Restaurant · Brandon & Riverview

Kojak's House of Ribs

A family-owned barbecue restaurant, serving since 1978, that moved from South Tampa to Seffner in 2022.

Around the Bay · Beach · New Port Richey & West Pasco

ABHillsborough River State Park

Around the Bay · State park · Plant City

Hillsborough River State Park

A state park near Thonotosassa where the Hillsborough River runs over Class II rapids, which the park notes are rare in Florida; it has four hiking trails totaling about 7.3 miles, canoe, kayak and bike rentals, a 112-site campground, picnic pavilions built by the Civilian Conservation Corps in the 1930s, and the interpretive center for Fort Foster State Historic Site.

Daily, 8 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. · $6 per vehicle.

ABAlafia River State Park

Around the Bay · State park · Brandon & Riverview

Alafia River State Park

A state park on a reclaimed phosphate mine near Lithia with some of the sharpest elevation changes in Florida, known for mountain-bike trails from beginner to highly advanced and a full-service bike shop; it also has about 20 miles of hilly hiking and horse trails, canoeing and fishing on the South Prong of the Alafia River, and a 30-site campground with 12 equestrian sites.

Daily, 8 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. · $5 per vehicle.

ABCockroach Bay Preserve State Park

Around the Bay · State park · Apollo Beach & Ruskin

Cockroach Bay Preserve State Park

A preserve of 617 acres of islands, mostly mangrove swamp, at the mouth of the Little Manatee River in southeastern Tampa Bay, reached only by private boat, canoe or kayak; two paddling trails wind through the surrounding aquatic preserve, and anglers and birders use it. The islands have no facilities; the nearest public boat ramp is at the end of Cockroach Bay Road.

Daily, 8 a.m. to sunset. · No fee.

ABLittle Manatee River State Park

Around the Bay · State park · Apollo Beach & Ruskin

Little Manatee River State Park

A state park near Wimauma on the Little Manatee River, a designated Outstanding Florida Water, used for canoeing and kayaking (with a launch at the River Steps), fishing and horseback riding; it has a 6.5-mile wilderness hiking loop in the north half, more than 15 miles of equestrian and multi-use trails in the south half, riverside picnic pavilions and a 30-site campground.

Daily, 8 a.m. to sunset, 365 days a year. · $5 per vehicle.
