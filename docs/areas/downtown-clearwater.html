Sheet 4 · Clearwater & North Pinellas · City

Downtown Clearwater

The mainland City of Clearwater, across the Clearwater Memorial Causeway from the beach, with a walkable downtown, Coachman Park and its BayCare Sound amphitheater, the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre and the Clearwater Marine Aquarium, which cares for rescued dolphins and sea turtles. The Philadelphia Phillies hold spring training here, and Ruth Eckerd Hall and the Countryside area are included in this part of the guide.

Known for Clearwater Marine Aquarium · Coachman Park and The BayCare Sound · the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre · Ruth Eckerd Hall · Philadelphia Phillies spring training

Historic sites here

  • 14Harbor Oaks Residential DistrictHistoric district

    An early 20th-century residential district on Clearwater Harbor just south of downtown Clearwater, developed by Dean Alvord.

  • 15Old Pinellas County CourthouseHistoric site · built 1917, 1924, 1926

    Pinellas County's Classical Revival courthouse in downtown Clearwater, designed by Francis J. Kennard, with its 1917 building and 1920s additions.
