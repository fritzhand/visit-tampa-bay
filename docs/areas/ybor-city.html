Sheet 1 · Tampa · District

Ybor City

Tampa's historic cigar-making district and a National Historic Landmark District, where converted brick cigar factories hold restaurants, cigar shops, museums and 7th Avenue's bars and music venues. The TECO Line Streetcar runs here from downtown, and free-roaming chickens have walked its streets for over a century.

Known for cigar factories and hand-rolled cigars · the Columbia Restaurant · the Ybor City Museum State Park · nightlife on 7th Avenue · the free-roaming Ybor chickens · the TECO Line Streetcar

Historic sites here

  • 131937 Antifascist Women's March marker and muralLandmark · built 2023 (marker)

    A 2023 marker and a 'No pasarán' mural on 7th Avenue recalling May 6, 1937, when thousands of Ybor City Latinas marched to City Hall against fascism in Spain.

  • 14Centro Asturiano de TampaHistoric site · built 1913–1914

    The clubhouse of a mutual-aid society founded in 1902 by immigrants from Asturias, Spain, completed in 1913–1914 with a theater, ballroom and cantina.

  • 15Cuban Club (El Círculo Cubano de Tampa)Historic site · built 1917

    The four-story clubhouse of El Círculo Cubano, a Cuban mutual-aid society founded in 1902, built in 1917 after fire destroyed the first clubhouse.

  • 16El Centro Español de TampaHistoric site · built 1912

    The 1912 clubhouse of El Centro Español, the mutual-aid society Spanish immigrants incorporated in 1891, and a National Historic Landmark.

  • 17El Pasaje (Cherokee Club)Historic site · built 1895 (NHL nomination; the NRHP record gives 1886)

    A two-story red brick building across 9th Avenue from the Ybor factory, built for the Cherokee Club, a men's social club whose guests included Grover Cleveland.

  • 18Home Association (Old People's Home)Historic site · built 1924

    A 1924 Colonial Revival home for the elderly, built on land donated by Peter O. Knight for a charity founded in 1899 after two elderly women arrived at Tampa's train…

  • 19Italian Club of Tampa (L'Unione Italiana)Historic site · built 1917–1918

    The 1918 clubhouse of L'Unione Italiana, the mutual-aid society Italian immigrants founded in Ybor City in 1894.

  • 20La Unión Martí-MaceoHistoric site · built 1950

    The clubhouse of Tampa's Afro-Cuban mutual-aid society, named for Cuban patriots José Martí and Antonio Maceo.

  • 21Our Lady of Perpetual Help ChurchHouse of worship · built 1937

    Ybor City's Catholic church, a buff brick Romanesque Revival building of 1937 that replaced a wooden church of 1891, which had served Catholics of all nationalities…

  • 22Palmetto Beach Historic DistrictHistoric district

    A National Register residential district just south of Ybor City, platted in 1894, where four cigar factories were built, the first in 1895.

  • 23Parque Amigos de José MartíHistoric site · built 1960

    A small walled park on the site of the home of Ruperto and Paulina Pedroso, Black Cubans who sheltered José Martí in 1893.

  • 24V. M. Ybor Cigar Factory (Ybor Factory Building)Historic site · built 1886 (additions 1895 and 1902)

    The red brick cigar factory Vicente Martínez-Ybor completed in 1886, the first factory of the planned town of Ybor City.

  • 25Ybor City Historic DistrictSignatureHistoric district · built 1886–1940

    A National Historic Landmark district northeast of downtown Tampa, founded in 1886 as a planned cigar-making town.
