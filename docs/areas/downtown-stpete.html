Sheet 2 · St. Petersburg · District

Downtown St. Petersburg

St. Petersburg's downtown on Tampa Bay, where Beach Drive and a string of bayfront parks lead to the 26-acre St. Pete Pier, and museums including the Dalí and the Museum of Fine Arts are within walking distance. Central Avenue runs west through the Central Arts and EDGE districts, with murals, galleries, bars and music venues such as Jannus Live.

Known for the St. Pete Pier · the Dalí Museum · the Museum of Fine Arts · Beach Drive restaurants · murals and galleries on Central Avenue · the Saturday Morning Market

Historic sites here

  • 30Detroit HotelHistoric site

    The building on the 200 block of Central Avenue that the city marks as the site of St. Petersburg's first hotel, the Detroit.

  • 31Downtown St. Petersburg Historic DistrictHistoric district

    The National Register district covering much of downtown St. Petersburg between 5th Avenue North, Beach Drive, Central Avenue and 9th Street, with 367 historic buildings.

  • 32Mirror Lake Community LibraryHistoric site · built 1915

    St. Petersburg's Carnegie library, opened on Mirror Lake in 1915 and still a branch of the city library system.

  • 33Open Air Post OfficeHistoric site · built 1916–1917

    St. Petersburg's 1916–1917 post office at First Avenue North and Fourth Street, known as the Open Air Post Office.

  • 34Snell ArcadeLandmark · built 1928

    A 1928 arcade building on Central Avenue developed by C. Perry Snell and designed by Richard Kiehnel of Kiehnel and Elliott.

  • 35The ColiseumHistoric site · built 1924

    City-owned 1924 ballroom in downtown St. Petersburg used for dances, trade shows, maker markets, banquets and private events; it seats 1,100 for a banquet without a…

  • World's First Airline MonumentLandmark · built 1938 (first plaque); 1953 (second plaque) · not on the map

    A stone monument with bronze plaques at the St. Pete Pier marking where the St. Petersburg–Tampa Airboat Line began scheduled flights across Tampa Bay on January 1, 1914.
