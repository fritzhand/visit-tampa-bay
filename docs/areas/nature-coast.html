Weeki Wachee & the Nature Coast

The spring country north of Tampa in Hernando and Citrus counties. At Weeki Wachee Springs State Park, mermaids have performed underwater shows in a first-magnitude spring since 1947, next to Buccaneer Bay, Florida's only spring-fed water park; farther north, Crystal River, which calls itself the Manatee Capital of the World, and Homosassa Springs Wildlife State Park are known for manatees. Brooksville, the Hernando County seat founded in 1856, has brick streets, murals and mermaid statues.

Known for Weeki Wachee mermaid shows · Buccaneer Bay · kayaking the Weeki Wachee River · swimming with manatees in Crystal River · Homosassa Springs Wildlife State Park · Brooksville's historic downtown
