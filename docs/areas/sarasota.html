Sheet 6 · Day Trips · City

Sarasota

A Gulf Coast city and county known for the white-sand beaches of Siesta Key and Lido Key, the shops and restaurants of St. Armands Circle, an active arts scene and The Ringling, the John and Mable Ringling Museum of Art. Visit Sarasota's area also covers Longboat Key, Casey Key, Venice, Englewood, North Port and Lakewood Ranch.

Known for Siesta Key beach · Lido Key · St. Armands Circle · The Ringling museum · arts and theater · Myakka parks and trails

    Pizzeria in Sarasota's Gulf Gate area from chef Anthony Petralia, who began with a one-day-a-week pop-up; it serves Detroit-style and round pies.

    Amish home-cooking restaurant in Sarasota's Pinecraft area, known for its pies, with a gift shop, produce market, deli and coffee-and-pie market on the same grounds.
