Sheet 1 · Tampa · District

East Tampa & the Fairgrounds

The city's East Tampa neighborhoods, north of I-4 and east of I-275, and the Orient Road and US 301 area beyond them. Visitors come here for the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino and the Florida State Fairgrounds, home of the Florida State Fair, the MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre and the Cracker Country living history museum.

Known for Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino Tampa · the Florida State Fair · MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre · Cracker Country

Historic sites here

  • 8Dr. Jacob White, Sr. HouseHistoric site · built 1925

    A 1925 two-story brick Craftsman house built by Dr. Jacob White Sr., the first Black general practitioner in Tampa, and later home of his son, a tuberculosis physician.

  • 9Memorial Park CemeteryCemetery · built 1919

    A 20-acre cemetery founded in 1919 for African American World War I veterans, which became the main burial ground for Tampa's Black community under segregation.
