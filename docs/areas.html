Sheet 1 · TP

Tampa

The Tampa sheet

13 areas · 228 places · 107 places to stay · 243 events

1Tampa · District

Downtown Tampa

Tampa's central business district, along the Hillsborough River. The 2.6-mile Tampa Riverwalk links its parks, museums and theaters, and the University of Tampa campus in the West Riverfront neighborhood holds the Henry B. Plant Museum.

the Tampa Riverwalk · Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park · Tampa Theatre · the Straz Center · Tampa Museum of Art · the Henry B. Plant Museum at the University of Tampa

44 places · 13 places to stay · 20 experiences · 53 events

2Tampa · District

Channel District & Water Street

A former port warehouse district that has become downtown Tampa's most densely residential neighborhood, home to the Florida Aquarium, Sparkman Wharf and the cruise ship terminals. Water Street Tampa, a development of more than 16 city blocks, adds hotels, Benchmark International Arena and the Tampa Bay History Center.

the Florida Aquarium · Sparkman Wharf · cruise ship terminals · Benchmark International Arena · the Tampa Bay History Center · the American Victory Ship & Museum

16 places · 7 places to stay · 6 experiences · 77 events

3Tampa · Island

Harbour Island

A 177-acre island across the Garrison Channel from downtown, once a rail-served phosphate terminal and developed since 1985 with homes, offices and the Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside hotel. The Tampa Convention Center is across the Harbour Island Bridge, and the Channel District is within walking distance.

the Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside · The Pointe at Harbour Island · the Harbour Island Bridge to the Tampa Convention Center

1 place · 1 place to stay · 1 experience

4Tampa · Island

Davis Islands

Two man-made islands at the mouth of the Hillsborough River, dredged and filled in the 1920s land boom by developer D. P. Davis, with many of the original Mediterranean-style buildings still standing. East Davis Boulevard has shops and cafes, a 1.3-mile trail runs to Peter O. Knight Airport, and Tampa General Hospital fills the northern tip.

1920s Mediterranean-style buildings · shops and cafes on East Davis Boulevard · the Davis Islands Trail · Peter O. Knight Airport and its seaplane basin · the Davis Island Yacht Club

4 places · 1 experience · 1 event

5Tampa · District

Ybor City

Tampa's historic cigar-making district and a National Historic Landmark District, where converted brick cigar factories hold restaurants, cigar shops, museums and 7th Avenue's bars and music venues. The TECO Line Streetcar runs here from downtown, and free-roaming chickens have walked its streets for over a century.

cigar factories and hand-rolled cigars · the Columbia Restaurant · the Ybor City Museum State Park · nightlife on 7th Avenue · the free-roaming Ybor chickens · the TECO Line Streetcar

39 places · 4 places to stay · 10 experiences · 11 events

6Tampa · Neighborhood

Tampa Heights

Founded in the 1880s as Tampa's first suburb, Tampa Heights lies just north of downtown between I-275, the Hillsborough River and Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard. Its riverfront has Armature Works, a restored historic building with a food hall and restaurants, and Water Works Park.

Armature Works · Water Works Park · bungalow-lined streets · coffee shops and breweries

15 places · 1 place to stay · 3 experiences · 1 event

7Tampa · Neighborhood

Hyde Park & SoHo

A historic neighborhood of homes and bungalows first built in the 1880s, next to the University of Tampa and bounded by Bayshore Boulevard and its 4.5-mile waterfront sidewalk. It includes Hyde Park Village, a shopping district of local shops and restaurants, and SoHo, the bar and restaurant strip on South Howard Avenue.

Bayshore Boulevard · Hyde Park Village · SoHo bars and restaurants on South Howard Avenue · historic homes and bungalows

13 places · 3 places to stay · 1 experience · 7 events

8Tampa · Neighborhood

West Tampa

Founded in 1892 as a cigar-making town and settled mostly by Cuban immigrants, West Tampa was its own city until Tampa annexed it in 1925; it lies west of the Hillsborough River about a mile from downtown. This area of the guide also takes in Raymond James Stadium, home of the Buccaneers, and George M. Steinbrenner Field, the Yankees' spring training ballpark, which face each other across Dale Mabry Highway.

cigar-era history · Cuban food · Raymond James Stadium and its pirate ship · George M. Steinbrenner Field · the 1914 West Tampa Free Public Library

21 places · 1 place to stay · 4 experiences · 45 events

9Tampa · Neighborhood

Seminole Heights

A streetcar suburb begun in 1911 about three miles north of downtown, known for its early 20th-century Craftsman bungalows and a local and national historic district. Restaurants, brewpubs and independent shops have opened here in recent years, and its parks give it the city's longest stretch of Hillsborough River parkland.

Craftsman bungalows · restaurants and brewpubs · riverfront parks on the Hillsborough River · the two-headed alligator statue on Nebraska Avenue

7 places · 1 event

10Tampa · District

North Tampa

The northern part of the city and its suburbs, with Busch Gardens Tampa Bay and its sister water park Adventure Island, ZooTampa at Lowry Park by the Hillsborough River, and MOSI, which has a pedestrian bridge to the University of South Florida. Includes the historic Sulphur Springs neighborhood, New Tampa and Carrollwood.

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Adventure Island · ZooTampa at Lowry Park · MOSI and its planetarium · the University of South Florida

26 places · 16 places to stay · 8 experiences · 30 events

11Tampa · District

Westshore & Airport

A principal business district of Tampa that takes in Tampa International Airport, with office towers, many hotels and two major malls, International Plaza and WestShore Plaza. Includes Rocky Point, with hotels and restaurants at the Tampa end of the Courtney Campbell Causeway to Clearwater, and Town 'n' Country.

Tampa International Airport · International Plaza · WestShore Plaza · airport-area hotels · Rocky Point on the Courtney Campbell Causeway

14 places · 44 places to stay · 2 experiences

12Tampa · District

South Tampa

The south end of the city below Hyde Park, reached along Bayshore Boulevard, whose waterfront path runs to Gandy Boulevard. Ballast Point Park looks across Hillsborough Bay to downtown, Palma Ceia has brick streets named for streets in Havana, and Picnic Island Park in Port Tampa, at the city's southwestern tip, has a sand beach, a dog beach and a kayak launch nearby.

Ballast Point Park · Picnic Island Park and its beach · Palma Ceia's brick streets · the Bayshore Boulevard greenway

15 places · 7 places to stay

13Tampa · District

East Tampa & the Fairgrounds

The city's East Tampa neighborhoods, north of I-4 and east of I-275, and the Orient Road and US 301 area beyond them. Visitors come here for the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino and the Florida State Fairgrounds, home of the Florida State Fair, the MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre and the Cracker Country living history museum.

Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino Tampa · the Florida State Fair · MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre · Cracker Country

13 places · 10 places to stay · 2 experiences · 17 events

Sheet 2 · SP

St. Petersburg

The St. Petersburg sheet

7 areas · 105 places · 48 places to stay · 129 events

1St. Pete · District

Downtown St. Petersburg

St. Petersburg's downtown on Tampa Bay, where Beach Drive and a string of bayfront parks lead to the 26-acre St. Pete Pier, and museums including the Dalí and the Museum of Fine Arts are within walking distance. Central Avenue runs west through the Central Arts and EDGE districts, with murals, galleries, bars and music venues such as Jannus Live.

the St. Pete Pier · the Dalí Museum · the Museum of Fine Arts · Beach Drive restaurants · murals and galleries on Central Avenue · the Saturday Morning Market

52 places · 17 places to stay · 25 experiences · 92 events

2St. Pete · District

Grand Central & Warehouse Arts District

West of downtown, the Grand Central District runs along Central Avenue from 16th to 31st Street with galleries, shops, breweries and bars; Visit St. Pete/Clearwater calls it the city's original LGBTQ+ neighborhood and the birthplace of St. Pete Pride. Just south, the Warehouse Arts District fills former industrial buildings with artists' studios, galleries and breweries. The guide also files Tropicana Field here: the Rays' domed ballpark reopened on April 6, 2026, after repairs to roof damage from Hurricane Milton.

LGBTQ+ bars and shops on Central Avenue · Warehouse Arts District studios and galleries · the Imagine Museum · craft breweries · Tropicana Field

19 places · 4 places to stay · 9 experiences · 27 events

3St. Pete · Neighborhood

Old Northeast & Snell Isle

The North Shore Historic District, known as Historic Old Northeast, is a mostly residential neighborhood next to downtown with nearly 3,000 historic buildings between 4th Street North and Tampa Bay. It holds Sunken Gardens, a botanical garden more than a century old that the city now runs, and bayfront parks; Snell Isle, developed on a mangrove island in the 1920s, and Crescent Lake are included here.

historic homes of the North Shore Historic District · Sunken Gardens · North Shore Park and its beach · Snell Isle

7 places · 1 place to stay · 1 experience

4St. Pete · District

South St. Pete & Midtown

The south side of St. Petersburg includes Midtown and 22nd Street South, known as The Deuces, once the city's Black Main Street, where the Dr. Carter G. Woodson African American Museum and the Historic Manhattan Casino stand. Farther south, Boyd Hill Nature Preserve covers 400 acres connected to Lake Maggiore, with trails through habitat for gopher tortoises and alligators.

The Deuces on 22nd Street South · the Woodson African American Museum · the Historic Manhattan Casino · Boyd Hill Nature Preserve · Lake Maggiore

12 places · 2 places to stay · 1 experience

5St. Pete · District

Northeast St. Pete & Weedon Island

The northeast corner of St. Petersburg on Tampa Bay, where the Gandy Bridge crosses Old Tampa Bay to Tampa. Its main draw is Weedon Island Preserve, a 3,000-acre county preserve with more than 4.7 miles of boardwalks and trails, paddling trails, a fishing pier and a cultural and natural history center, on a site where Indigenous peoples lived for thousands of years. Includes Gateway and Carillon.

Weedon Island Preserve · boardwalks and paddling trails · the Weedon Island Preserve Cultural and Natural History Center · the Gandy Bridge to Tampa

2 places · 22 places to stay · 1 experience · 1 event

6St. Pete · District

West St. Pete

St. Petersburg's western neighborhoods, including Tyrone, around the Tyrone Square mall, Azalea and Jungle Prada, where a city park holds part of an archaeological site with Tocobaga mounds, listed on the National Register in 2003 and identified as the landing place of the Narváez expedition. freeFall Theatre stages plays and musicals on Central Avenue, and St. Petersburg College's Gibbs Campus planetarium gives free public shows on Wednesday and Friday evenings while the college is in session.

Tyrone Square mall · the Jungle Prada mound site · freeFall Theatre · free shows at the SPC Planetarium

6 places · 5 events

7St. Pete · City

Gulfport

A small city on Boca Ciega Bay bordering St. Petersburg, with a walkable waterfront district of independent boutiques, galleries and restaurants along Beach and Shore boulevards. The Gulfport Casino, a waterfront ballroom whose current building dates from the 1930s, is on the National Register, and the Gulfport Tuesday Fresh Market runs year-round.

independent boutiques and galleries · the Gulfport Casino · the Gulfport Tuesday Fresh Market · the Gulfport Night Market · Clam Bayou Nature Preserve

7 places · 2 places to stay · 1 experience · 4 events

Sheet 3 · GB

Gulf Beaches

The Gulf Beaches sheet

8 areas · 62 places · 138 places to stay · 13 events

1Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Clearwater Beach

The City of Clearwater's beach, on a barrier island joined to the mainland by the Clearwater Memorial Causeway, with about 2.5 miles of white quartz sand and calm, shallow water just steps from hotels and restaurants. Pier 60 anchors the beach and hosts the nightly Sunsets at Pier 60 celebration with street performers and vendors.

white quartz sand · Pier 60 and Sunsets at Pier 60 · pirate, dolphin and dinner cruises · parasailing and jet skis · beachfront resorts

16 places · 35 places to stay · 18 experiences · 4 events

2Gulf Beaches · Island

Sand Key & Belleair Beach

Sand Key is the barrier island that runs from Clearwater south to Madeira Beach; this area covers its northern end, joined to Clearwater Beach by a short bridge. The 95-acre Sand Key Park in Clearwater has a white sand beach with seasonal lifeguards and sea turtle nesting from April to October, and the small cities of Belleair Beach and Belleair Shore lie to its south.

Sand Key Park · sea turtle nesting · salt marsh views

2 places · 3 places to stay · 1 event

3Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Indian Rocks Beach & Indian Shores

A long, quieter stretch of beach towns between Clearwater Beach and Madeira Beach, where mom-and-pop shops, small motels and vacation rentals line three miles of gently sloping beach in Indian Rocks Beach, and the long shoreline suits walks and shelling. Indian Shores has the Seaside Seabird Sanctuary, and this area also covers North Redington Beach and Redington Shores.

quiet beaches for shelling · small motels and vacation rentals · the Seaside Seabird Sanctuary · the Indian Rocks Historical Museum · beachside restaurants and bars

13 places · 17 places to stay

4Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Madeira Beach & John's Pass

A barrier island town that Visit St. Pete/Clearwater likens to an Old Florida fishing village crossed with a beach town, with about two miles of beach. John's Pass Village & Boardwalk, on the waterfront at John's Pass, has more than 100 merchants, including shops and restaurants, the local fishing fleet, and dolphin, shelling, parasail and jet ski outfitters; Redington Beach, a small residential town, is included here.

John's Pass Village & Boardwalk · fishing charters and the local fishing fleet · dolphin and shelling tours · Hubbard's Marina

7 places · 7 places to stay · 9 experiences · 2 events

5Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Treasure Island

A barrier island city between St. Pete Beach and Madeira Beach with nearly four miles of extra-wide white-sand beach in three main areas: Sunshine Beach, Municipal Beach and Sunset Beach. Visit St. Pete/Clearwater describes it as a more residential, laid-back stretch of coast; a beach trail runs along the sand, and an international sand sculpting festival is held every fall.

extra-wide white-sand beaches · Sunset Beach · the Treasure Island Beach Trail · a fall sand sculpting festival · ADA beach mats

6 places · 35 places to stay · 1 experience · 1 event

6Gulf Beaches · Beach town

St. Pete Beach

A beach city on seven miles of Gulf shoreline just west of downtown St. Petersburg, with wide sandy beaches, warm shallow water, beachfront resorts and the landmark Don CeSar hotel. Its beach bar scene gathers around Corey Avenue, and the historic Pass-a-Grille district is at its southern tip.

the Don CeSar · Upham Beach Park · Corey Avenue · beachfront resorts · water sports outfitters

7 places · 31 places to stay · 4 experiences · 5 events

7Gulf Beaches · Beach town

Pass-a-Grille

The historic southern tip of St. Pete Beach and one of the area's quietest beach communities, where Visit St. Pete/Clearwater notes there is almost no building directly on the sand. 8th Avenue has pastel-colored shops, galleries and restaurants, the Merry Pier is a historic fishing spot, and a shuttle runs to the undeveloped Shell Key Preserve.

sunsets on the beach · 8th Avenue shops and galleries · the Merry Pier · the Shell Key Shuttle

6 places · 7 places to stay · 1 experience

8Gulf Beaches · Island

Fort De Soto & Tierra Verde

Fort De Soto, the largest park in the Pinellas County park system, covers 1,136 acres on five connected islands, with beaches, a 236-site campground, a dog beach, a 6.8-mile paved trail and the fort's 12-inch mortar battery, listed on the National Register in 1977. Ferries run from the park to Egmont Key and Shell Key, and Tierra Verde, the boating community on the way in, has marinas, fishing spots and casual restaurants.

Fort De Soto Park beaches · the historic fort and mortar battery · camping · ferries to Egmont Key and Shell Key · Tierra Verde marinas

5 places · 3 places to stay · 5 experiences

Sheet 4 · CW

Clearwater & North Pinellas

The Clearwater & North Pinellas sheet

7 areas · 82 places · 38 places to stay · 134 events

1Clearwater · City

Downtown Clearwater

The mainland City of Clearwater, across the Clearwater Memorial Causeway from the beach, with a walkable downtown, Coachman Park and its BayCare Sound amphitheater, the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre and the Clearwater Marine Aquarium, which cares for rescued dolphins and sea turtles. The Philadelphia Phillies hold spring training here, and Ruth Eckerd Hall and the Countryside area are included in this part of the guide.

Clearwater Marine Aquarium · Coachman Park and The BayCare Sound · the Bilheimer Capitol Theatre · Ruth Eckerd Hall · Philadelphia Phillies spring training

17 places · 7 places to stay · 9 experiences · 88 events

2Clearwater · City

Dunedin

A small, Scottish-influenced city just north of Clearwater with a walkable, bike-friendly downtown of independent boutiques, cafes and breweries on the Pinellas Trail. The Dunedin Causeway leads to Honeymoon Island State Park, where a ferry leaves for Caladesi Island State Park, and the Toronto Blue Jays train each spring at TD Ballpark.

downtown breweries, including Dunedin Brewery · Honeymoon Island State Park · Caladesi Island State Park · Scottish heritage · Toronto Blue Jays spring training at TD Ballpark · the Pinellas Trail

16 places · 8 places to stay · 3 experiences · 21 events

3Clearwater · City

Safety Harbor

A small city on Old Tampa Bay with brick streets under moss-draped oaks, a Main Street of restaurants and shops, and the historic Safety Harbor Resort & Spa, established in 1925 on natural mineral springs once believed to be the Fountain of Youth. Philippe Park, the oldest park in Pinellas County, holds the Tocobaga Temple Mound, the largest remaining mound in the Tampa Bay region.

the Safety Harbor Resort & Spa · its natural springs · Main Street restaurants and craft beer · the Tocobaga Temple Mound at Philippe Park · the Safety Harbor Museum & Cultural Center

8 places · 3 places to stay · 2 experiences · 7 events

4Clearwater · City

Tarpon Springs

A Gulf coast city about 30 minutes north of Clearwater whose sponge industry, worked by more than 500 Greek sponge divers by 1905, built the Greek community it is known for. The Sponge Docks along pedestrian-friendly Dodecanese Boulevard still draw visitors to sponge boats, shops and Greek restaurants; Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says the city has the largest Greek community per capita in the United States.

the Sponge Docks · Greek restaurants on Dodecanese Boulevard · natural sea sponges · Fred Howard Park · Anclote Key Preserve State Park

23 places · 4 places to stay · 3 experiences · 7 events

5Clearwater · County area

Palm Harbor & Innisbrook

An unincorporated community about 20 minutes north of Clearwater, with a small downtown of shops, eateries and historic buildings. Innisbrook Golf Resort's Copperhead course hosts the PGA Tour's annual Valspar Championship, and John Chesnut Sr. Park in the East Lake area and Wall Springs Park are its best-known parks.

Innisbrook Golf Resort · the Valspar Championship on the Copperhead course · downtown Palm Harbor and the Palm Harbor Museum · John Chesnut Sr. Park · Wall Springs Park

5 places · 2 places to stay

6Clearwater · City

Oldsmar

A small city at the top of Old Tampa Bay that Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says has more parks per square mile than any other city in Florida, including Mobbly Bayou Wilderness Preserve and R.E. Olds Park. The Tampa Bay Downs thoroughbred racetrack is in Oldsmar, as is the Oldsmar Flea Market.

Tampa Bay Downs · Mobbly Bayou Wilderness Preserve · R.E. Olds Park · the Oldsmar Flea Market

2 places · 4 places to stay · 1 experience · 4 events

7Clearwater · City

Largo & Mid-Pinellas

Largo is a largely residential city with a natural side: the Florida Botanical Gardens and Heritage Village, Largo Central Park with the Largo Central Railroad, and parks, trails and public golf courses. This area also covers Seminole, a gateway to Madeira Beach and Indian Shores with Lake Seminole Park; Pinellas Park, with the Tampa Bay Automobile Museum and nearby Sawgrass Lake Park; and Belleair and Kenneth City.

Florida Botanical Gardens · Heritage Village · Largo Central Park · Lake Seminole Park · Sawgrass Lake Park · the Pinellas Trail

11 places · 10 places to stay · 1 experience · 7 events

Around the Bay

1Around the Bay · City

Temple Terrace

A small city next to Tampa in northeastern Hillsborough County, founded in 1920 as one of the first planned golf-course communities in the United States, with Mediterranean Revival architecture, and named for the Temple orange. The Hillsborough River runs through the city, and Florida College occupies some of its historic buildings.

1920s Mediterranean Revival architecture · its historic golf course · the Hillsborough River and Riverhills Drive · Florida College

1 place · 8 places to stay

2Around the Bay · County area

Brandon & Riverview

A large unincorporated suburb about 11 miles east of downtown Tampa, with the Brandon Exchange mall and its more than 140 stores. The area also takes in Lithia Springs Park, where a spring that stays at 72 degrees allows swimming all year, and Alafia River State Park, with 17 miles of mountain bike trails. Includes Riverview, Valrico and Seffner.

Brandon Exchange mall · Lithia Springs Park · Alafia River State Park mountain biking

5 places · 26 places to stay · 1 event

3Around the Bay · County area

Apollo Beach & Ruskin

Unincorporated waterfront communities on Tampa Bay south of Tampa. Apollo Beach has about 55 miles of canals and Tampa Electric's Manatee Viewing Center, where manatees gather in winter in the warm discharge canal of the Big Bend Power Station; the center is open daily from November 1 to April 15. E.G. Simmons Regional Park, north of Ruskin, has a bay beach, a mangrove bird sanctuary, a fishing pier and kayak rentals. Includes Ruskin, Gibsonton and Sun City Center.

the Manatee Viewing Center · E.G. Simmons Regional Park · boating and fishing canals · the Big Bend Power Station

5 places · 6 places to stay · 3 experiences

4Around the Bay · City

Plant City

A small city between Tampa and Orlando, founded in 1885 as the railroad arrived and named for Henry B. Plant, known for its strawberry industry. The Florida Strawberry Festival is held here each spring, and the historic downtown hosts events such as Oktoberfest, Last Friday and the Strawberry Drop. Includes Dover and Thonotosassa, home of Hillsborough River State Park.

strawberries · the Florida Strawberry Festival · historic downtown · Dinosaur World · Hillsborough River State Park

14 places · 8 places to stay · 1 experience · 8 events

5Around the Bay · County area

Wesley Chapel & East Pasco

South and east Pasco County, north of Tampa. Wesley Chapel has The Shops at Wiregrass, Tampa Premium Outlets, the Epperson Lagoon swimming complex and Saddlebrook Resort; Zephyrhills is known for Skydive City and its World War II airfield museum, and Dade City for a historic downtown around its courthouse and for antique shopping. Includes Lutz, Land O' Lakes and Odessa.

The Shops at Wiregrass · Tampa Premium Outlets · Saddlebrook Resort · Skydive City in Zephyrhills · Dade City's historic downtown and antiques · the Pioneer Florida Museum

12 places · 19 places to stay · 4 experiences · 4 events

6Around the Bay · County area

New Port Richey & West Pasco

Pasco County's Gulf coast, centered on New Port Richey, whose walkable downtown on the Cotee River has Sims Park, the restored Hacienda hotel where Hollywood stars once stayed, and the 1925 Richey Suncoast Theatre. Kayak rentals run through the river's mangrove tunnels. Includes Port Richey, Holiday and Hudson.

The Hacienda · Sims Park · kayaking the Cotee River · the Richey Suncoast Theatre

9 places · 12 places to stay · 1 event

Day Trips

The Day Trips sheet

1Day Trips · County area

Bradenton & Anna Maria Island

The Bradenton Area pairs the city of Bradenton, with its riverwalk, the Village of the Arts and the Bishop Museum of Science and Nature, with Anna Maria Island's white-sand beaches, Pine Avenue shops and the historic Bridge Street Pier. Cortez is one of the oldest working fishing villages, the De Soto National Memorial sits on the Manatee River, and the Gulf Islands Ferry links downtown Bradenton and the island. Includes Palmetto, Ellenton and the north end of Longboat Key.

Anna Maria Island beaches · Pine Avenue · the Cortez fishing village · the Village of the Arts · the Bishop Museum of Science and Nature · the Gulf Islands Ferry

33 places · 1 place to stay · 4 experiences · 25 events

2Day Trips · City

Sarasota

A Gulf Coast city and county known for the white-sand beaches of Siesta Key and Lido Key, the shops and restaurants of St. Armands Circle, an active arts scene and The Ringling, the John and Mable Ringling Museum of Art. Visit Sarasota's area also covers Longboat Key, Casey Key, Venice, Englewood, North Port and Lakewood Ranch.

Siesta Key beach · Lido Key · St. Armands Circle · The Ringling museum · arts and theater · Myakka parks and trails

36 places · 3 places to stay · 3 experiences · 10 events

3Day Trips · County area

Weeki Wachee & the Nature Coast

The spring country north of Tampa in Hernando and Citrus counties. At Weeki Wachee Springs State Park, mermaids have performed underwater shows in a first-magnitude spring since 1947, next to Buccaneer Bay, Florida's only spring-fed water park; farther north, Crystal River, which calls itself the Manatee Capital of the World, and Homosassa Springs Wildlife State Park are known for manatees. Brooksville, the Hernando County seat founded in 1856, has brick streets, murals and mermaid statues.

Weeki Wachee mermaid shows · Buccaneer Bay · kayaking the Weeki Wachee River · swimming with manatees in Crystal River · Homosassa Springs Wildlife State Park · Brooksville's historic downtown

20 places · 6 experiences

4Day Trips · County area

Lakeland, Winter Haven & Polk County

Polk County, between Tampa and Orlando, with LEGOLAND Florida Resort, Bok Tower Gardens, Frank Lloyd Wright's 13 structures at Florida Southern College and 554 freshwater lakes for fishing, airboat tours and water sports. Includes Lakeland, Winter Haven and Lake Wales.

LEGOLAND Florida Resort · Bok Tower Gardens · Frank Lloyd Wright buildings at Florida Southern College · lakes and airboat tours · Westgate River Ranch

29 places · 2 experiences · 19 events
