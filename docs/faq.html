7 questions

When to visit

Weather by month
When is the best time to visit Tampa Bay?

Visit Tampa Bay calls the area a year-round destination. It says many visitors prefer November through April, when temperatures are milder and humidity is lower.

Source: visittampabay.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How warm is the Gulf?

Visit St. Pete/Clearwater lists average water temperatures of 64°F in January and February, 73°F in April, 82°F in June, 87°F in August, 81°F in October and 69°F in December.

Source: visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

When is high season on the St. Pete-Clearwater beaches?

Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says January through May is considered high season because of the weather. It says summer is popular with families, and that fall offers savings and fewer crowds.

Source: visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

When is the rainy season?

Summer. At Tampa International Airport, June through September each average more than 6 inches of rain, with August the wettest month (9.03 inches), while November averages 1.40 inches (NOAA 1991–2020 normals). The National Weather Service in Tampa Bay says the Florida peninsula tends to see thunderstorms most summer days.

Sources: ncei.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab) · weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How hot is it in summer?

Hot and humid. In the NOAA 1991–2020 normals for Tampa International Airport, average highs are 90.2°F to 91.2°F from June through September, and nights stay near 75°F to 77°F. Tampa averages 94.3 days a year with a high of 90°F or more.

Source: ncei.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

When does it cool off in the fall?

Usually later than the calendar suggests. The National Weather Service in Tampa Bay says highs in the mid 80s to low 90s and high humidity typically last well into October, that the first cool front brings only a day or two of relief, and that the real cool-down usually comes in November. On average Tampa sees its first night below 60°F on October 19 and St. Petersburg on October 30.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How warm is it in winter?

Mild. In the NOAA 1991–2020 normals for Tampa International Airport, January averages a high of 71.3°F and a low of 52.8°F, and December and February are a few degrees warmer. St. Petersburg, on the water, averages 69.0°F and 55.3°F in January.

Source: ncei.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Weather & hurricanes

Hurricane season
Where can I check the beach forecast?

The National Weather Service's beach forecast page for the Tampa Bay area shows the rip current risk, the UV index and water temperature. Its rip current risk is Low, Moderate or High; at High, it says life-threatening rip currents are likely and the surf zone is dangerous for all swimmers.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What happens if a hurricane threatens during my trip?

Follow local officials. Pinellas County orders evacuations by zone to protect people from storm surge, and everyone in a zone named in an order must leave. It lists a hotel outside the ordered area as one option, says you do not have to go far, and says Visit St. Pete/Clearwater shares accommodation information as a storm approaches. Check roads at fl511.com.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

When is hurricane season?

The Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, according to the National Hurricane Center. The Atlantic basin includes the Gulf.

Source: nhc.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What is the difference between a hurricane watch and a warning?

A hurricane watch means hurricane-force winds (74 mph or more) are possible in the area; the National Hurricane Center issues it 48 hours before tropical-storm-force winds are expected. A hurricane warning means those winds are expected, and it is issued 36 hours ahead. Storm surge watches and warnings work the same way for life-threatening flooding from the sea.

Source: nhc.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Did Hurricanes Helene and Milton hit Tampa Bay?

Yes, both in 2024. Helene, which made landfall in the Big Bend as a Category 4 in late September, pushed record storm surge into the bay area: 6.67 feet above the normal high-tide line at Clearwater Beach and 7.2 feet at Tampa's East Bay gauge, and the National Weather Service reported 12 storm-surge drownings in Pinellas County. Milton made landfall on the west-central Florida coast as a Category 3 in October, with a 101 mph gust and 18.54 inches of rain in one day at St. Petersburg's Albert Whitted Airport.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab) (5 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What if a storm comes up while I'm at the beach?

Get out of the water and off the beach, and go to your car. The National Weather Service says not to shelter under beach picnic shelters and to wait 30 minutes after the last rumble of thunder before going back to the beach. It also says most lightning injuries and deaths on boats happen on small boats with no cabin.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Is lightning a real risk in Florida?

Yes. As of September 22, 2026, the National Weather Service counted 16 lightning deaths in the United States in 2026, and 6 of them were in Florida. The figures are preliminary.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What should I do when I hear thunder?

Go inside. The National Weather Service says there is no safe place outside when thunderstorms are in the area, and that if you hear thunder you are likely within striking distance. Its rule: When Thunder Roars, Go Indoors.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

When is the peak of hurricane season?

The National Hurricane Center puts the peak on September 10, with most activity between mid-August and mid-October. It also notes that storms sometimes form before June 1 or after November 30.

Source: nhc.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Sun & heat

Sun, heat and lightning
What are the signs of heat illness?

The National Weather Service lists heavy sweating, cool and clammy skin, a fast weak pulse, cramps, dizziness or nausea as signs of heat exhaustion: get to a cool place and sip water. A body temperature above 103°F, hot red skin, confusion or fainting can mean heat stroke, a medical emergency: call 911.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Why does it feel hotter than the forecast?

Humidity. The heat index is the feels-like temperature, and the National Weather Service in Tampa Bay notes that forecast heat index values are for shade: in direct sun it can be up to 15°F higher. It says the most dangerous heat here comes when winds are from the west and thunderstorms cannot form.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I leave a child or pet in the car for a few minutes?

No. The National Weather Service says it is never safe to leave a child, disabled person or pet locked in a car, even in winter.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How strong is the sun?

Strong. The National Weather Service beach forecast for the Tampa Bay area includes a daily UV index; at 11 or more (Extreme) it warns that unprotected skin can burn in minutes.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Beaches & water safety

Beaches & outdoors
Where can I check conditions on the St. Pete-Clearwater beaches?

Visit St. Pete/Clearwater keeps a Beach Updates page with the current status (on September 27, 2026 it read: all St. Pete-Clearwater beaches are open, with no advisories), live beach webcams, an explanation of the warning flags, and a link to Mote Marine Laboratory's beach conditions map.

Source: visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What do the beach warning flags mean?

Florida uses one set of flags statewide. Green means low hazard (calm conditions, exercise caution). Yellow means medium hazard (moderate surf and/or currents). Red means high hazard (high surf and/or strong currents). Two red flags mean the water is closed to the public. Purple means stinging marine life, such as man o' war, jellyfish or stingrays. The state sign adds that the absence of flags does not assure safe waters.

Sources: floridadep.gov (opens in a new tab) · leg.state.fl.us (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Is the beach water tested for bacteria?

Yes. The Florida Department of Health's Healthy Beaches Program samples beach water for enterococci bacteria. If a sample and a resample both exceed 70 per 100 milliliters, it issues an advisory for that site. The City of Tampa asks visitors to its four bay beaches (Ben T. Davis, Picnic Island, Cypress Point and Davis Islands) to check the Healthy Beaches results before a trip.

Sources: floridahealth.gov (opens in a new tab) · tampa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Are the beaches open after Hurricanes Helene and Milton?

Yes. Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says all beaches and beach parks are open. Some park amenities are still being repaired; Pinellas County lists them on its County Parks: Know Before You Go page, last updated September 25, 2026 (for example, the far end of Fort De Soto's bay pier is closed for hurricane repairs).

Sources: visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab) (2 pages) · pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Are there lifeguards?

On Clearwater Beach, yes: the City of Clearwater says lifeguards are on duty seven days a week from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., with towers staffed by season and weather. Pinellas County lists seasonal lifeguards at Sand Key Park.

Sources: myclearwater.com (opens in a new tab) · pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How can I help protect manatees?

Report a sick or injured manatee to FWC's Wildlife Alert Hotline at 1-888-404-3922. Pinellas County also asks boaters and anglers to secure items on their vessels, keep plastic bags and fishing line in a container with a lid, and pick up any plastic or fishing line they see. The county enforces no-wake zones and seagrass protection areas.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Is seafood safe to eat during red tide?

Store-bought and restaurant shellfish are generally safe to eat during a bloom, FWC says, because the government monitors them. Recreational harvesting of clams, oysters and mussels is banned in areas closed for red tide, and cooking, heating or freezing does not get rid of the toxins.

Source: myfwc.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I swim during red tide?

FWC says not to swim in or around red tide: it can cause skin irritation, rashes and burning, sore eyes. When the bloom is present and winds blow onshore, some people cough, sneeze or feel eye, nose and throat irritation; offshore winds usually keep those effects down.

Source: myfwc.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What is red tide?

A bloom of a microscopic alga, Karenia brevis, that makes toxins. Pinellas County says red tide has been noted in Florida since the 1800s. It can affect fish and marine wildlife and irritate your eyes, nose and throat; the county says symptoms should go away when you leave the area, and people with asthma or chronic lung disease should avoid areas with an active bloom.

Sources: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) · myfwc.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Where can I check for red tide before I go?

The Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC) posts a statewide red tide status report on Friday afternoons, with extra information on Wednesdays, and a sampling map updated daily at 5 p.m. You can also call 866-300-9399 at any time for a recorded report. Pinellas County keeps a red tide page, and Visit St. Pete/Clearwater posts beach conditions.

Sources: myfwc.com (opens in a new tab) · pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) · visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Who do I call about a stranded sea turtle, manatee or a fish kill?

Call FWC's Wildlife Alert Hotline at 888-404-3922 for a dead, sick or injured manatee or sea turtle. Report fish kills to FWC's Fish Kill Hotline at 800-636-0511.

Sources: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) · visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What do I do if a rip current pulls me out?

Do not fight it. NOAA says to swim parallel to the shore and then swim back to land at an angle. Rip currents can move at up to eight feet per second, faster than an Olympic swimmer, and panicked swimmers who swim straight back to shore risk drowning from fatigue.

Source: oceanservice.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can rip currents happen on calm, sunny days?

Yes. The National Weather Service says rip currents often form on calm, sunny days. It advises checking the beach forecast, talking to the lifeguard, and swimming at a beach with lifeguards; it cites the U.S. Lifesaving Association's figure that the chance of drowning at a beach with lifeguards is 1 in 18 million.

Source: weather.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What should I know about sea turtle nesting season?

In Pinellas County, sea turtles nest and hatch from May 1 through October 31. The county asks beachgoers to turn off lights visible from the beach after sunset, pick up trash, fill in holes and knock down sandcastles when they leave, take all their beach equipment, and never harass or touch sea turtles or hatchlings. Report a turtle in trouble or a disturbed nest to FWC at (888) 404-3922.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What is the stingray shuffle?

Shuffling or sliding your feet slowly through the sand as you wade. The City of Clearwater says this warns stingrays that you are there, and they will move out of your way.

Source: myclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Where can I check today's water temperature?

The National Weather Service beach forecast page for the Tampa Bay area shows water temperature, and Visit St. Pete/Clearwater points to Mote Marine Laboratory's beach conditions map (visitbeaches.org), which lists water temperature, wind, tides and UV. NOAA notes that many people find water of 70°F to 78°F comfortable for swimming.

Sources: weather.gov (opens in a new tab) · visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab) · ncei.noaa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Every way to get around
Is there a bus along the Gulf beaches?

PSTA's Suncoast Beach Trolley runs daily along Gulf Boulevard from St. Pete Beach (75th Ave) through Treasure Island, John's Pass, Madeira Beach, the Redingtons, Indian Shores, Indian Rocks Beach and Sand Key to Clearwater Beach and downtown Clearwater's Park Street Terminal; the PSTA fare is $2.25. On Clearwater Beach itself, the Jolley Trolley ($2.25) runs North Beach and South Beach routes and a Coastal Route to Dunedin, Palm Harbor and Tarpon Springs.

DASH (Downtown Area Shared Hubs) is an app-hailed ride service run by the Tampa Downtown Partnership between more than twenty virtual hubs downtown, in all-electric vehicles, for $2 per person. It runs Monday to Saturday 7 AM-11 PM and Sunday 12 PM-9 PM.

Do I need a SunPass to drive around Tampa Bay?

No, but it is cheaper. The Tampa-area toll roads on Florida's Turnpike list (Veterans Expressway, Suncoast Parkway, I-4 Connector, Selmon Expressway and the Sunshine Skyway Bridge) do not accept cash. With SunPass, or an accepted transponder such as E-ZPass, E-PASS or Peach Pass, you pay the lowest toll rate; without one, Toll-By-Plate photographs the plate and mails the owner an invoice with a $2.50 administrative fee. Florida's Turnpike says SunPass saves an average of 25%.

Can I rent a bike or scooter in St. Petersburg?

Yes. The city's bike share uses Lime e-bikes, which you must end at a designated hub, and Spin and Lime rent e-scooters. Riders must be 18 or older, may not ride on sidewalks, and may ride to the St. Pete Pier but not on it; scooter corrals are at the base of the Pier.

The Short Term garage above the main terminal and the Long Term garage next to it charge nothing for the first hour, then $8 an hour, up to $32 and $26 per 24 hours. Economy Parking, linked by the SkyConnect train, is free for the first hour, then $5 an hour, up to $22 per 24 hours. Maximums include tax, stays are limited to 45 days, and the Cell Phone Waiting Lot is free.

Where do I pick up a rental car at Tampa International Airport?

At the Rental Car Center, reached by the SkyConnect train from the main terminal. Alamo, Avis, Budget, Dollar, Enterprise, Hertz, National, Thrifty, Ace, American, Fox, Payless, Sixt and Zipcar are there; off-airport companies pick up at the Rental Car Center's remote curb. Most domestic passengers can also check bags there from 5 a.m. to 5 p.m.

How much is a taxi from Tampa International Airport?

Yellow Cab serves the airport from the Ground Transportation Areas Red 1 and Blue 2, outside baggage claim. The airport lists the fare as $3.50 for the first 1/5 mile plus $2.85 a mile, with a $22 minimum from the airport and a $40 flat rate to downtown Tampa and the cruise terminal.

Source: tampaairport.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How do I get from Tampa International Airport to downtown Tampa without a car?

HART Routes 10 (Cypress St.) and 30 (Kennedy Blvd.) run between the airport and downtown Tampa seven days a week, and Route 32 connects the airport with the Netpark Transfer Center. The buses stop at the airport's Rental Car Center: take the SkyConnect train from the main terminal. A HART ride costs $2.00. A Yellow Cab taxi to downtown Tampa or the cruise terminal is a $40 flat rate, and Lyft, Uber and Wridz pick up at the Red Express or Blue Express curbside, with a $5.00 airport fee added to the fare.

Is there a bus from Tampa International Airport to St. Petersburg or Pinellas County?

PSTA says its new 727 Airport Express, between nine stops in downtown St. Petersburg and Tampa International Airport, launches October 4, 2026 and will run daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. (fare not yet published). Until then, and on weekdays after, PSTA's Route 300 express stops at the airport's Rental Car Facility on its way between downtown Tampa and the Ulmerton Park and Ride in Largo, Monday through Friday only, for the regular $2.25 fare.

Can I take a train to Tampa?

Amtrak serves Tampa Union Station (601 North Nebraska Avenue) with the Floridian, which Amtrak describes as a temporary combination of the Capitol Limited and Silver Star running daily between Chicago and Miami via Washington, DC.

Is there one transit pass for both Tampa and Pinellas buses?

HART sells a Month Passport for $85.00 that is valid on HART local, limited express and HARTFlex service and on PSTA buses and trolleys. Both agencies also use Flamingo Fares, with daily fare caps ($4.00 on HART, $5 on PSTA).

How much is beach parking on Treasure Island and Madeira Beach?

Treasure Island's city lots cost $5.00 an hour on weekdays and $5.50 on weekends and holidays ($6.00 and $6.50 on the Sunset Beach side streets); pay by text or with ParkMobile, and do not back into spaces, because license-plate readers enforce payment. Madeira Beach charges $4.00 an hour in its lots, paid through the ParkMobile app.

Is parking free in Ybor City?

The first two hours are free in the City of Tampa's Ybor lots, then $2 an hour up to $14 a day; a $10 flat rate applies Friday and Saturday from 8 p.m. to 3 a.m. Ybor street meters charge $3 for the first hour and $2 after.

Alcohol & rules

Can I drink alcohol on Clearwater Beach?

No. The City of Clearwater says no alcohol or other intoxicants are permitted on its public beaches. Its beach rules also list no smoking and no glass, and ban fires, bonfires and unpermitted fireworks.

Source: myclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Which Pinellas beaches allow alcohol?

It depends on the town. Visit St. Pete/Clearwater lists: no alcohol at Clearwater Beach, Honeymoon and Caladesi islands, Belleair Beach, Indian Rocks Beach, Fort De Soto Park or Gulfport Beach; alcohol allowed but not in glass at Madeira Beach and Treasure Island (Treasure Island bans it between the 8500 and 9900 blocks from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on Saturdays and Sundays); allowed at Redington Beach; and at St. Pete Beach only in beachfront hotels' cabana areas for registered guests.

Source: visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What is not allowed in Pinellas County beach parks?

Pinellas County's park rules prohibit alcohol, glass, grills and campfires on beaches, balloons of any kind, drones and other remote-controlled devices, and pets on beaches, boardwalks and fishing piers (Fort De Soto's dog beach is the exception). County parks are open 7 a.m. to sunset.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Where are the dog beaches in Pinellas County?

Fort De Soto Park is the only Pinellas County park where dogs are allowed on the beach, in a designated area, with two fenced areas for large and small dogs nearby. Visit St. Pete/Clearwater also lists the dog beach at the southern tip of Honeymoon Island State Park, where dogs must be on a six-foot handheld leash.

Sources: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) · visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Are there dog beaches in Tampa?

Yes: the City of Tampa runs the Davis Islands Dog Park and Dog Beach (1002 Severn St.) and the Picnic Island Dog Beach (7409 Picnic Island Blvd.). Dogs are not permitted on other city beaches unless they are service animals.

Source: tampa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I bring my dog to Clearwater Beach?

Not on the public bathing beach, except service animals. The City of Clearwater allows leashed dogs north of Somerset Street, but says that area is not a dog beach, and suggests a walk along Beach Walk instead.

Source: myclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I set off fireworks on the beach?

No. Visit St. Pete/Clearwater says Pinellas County bans the use and sale of fireworks that explode or project into the air; professional displays are held on July 4th and New Year's Eve.

Source: visitstpeteclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I drink or bring my dog at the St. Pete Pier?

Alcohol is allowed in the Pier District only in Pier-approved cups from its restaurants and cafes; you cannot bring your own. Leashed pets are welcome (leash six feet or less), but dogs are not allowed on Spa Beach or the splash pad. The Pier property closes at 11 p.m.

Source: stpetepier.org (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

8 questions

Safety

Are there alligators, and where is it safe to swim?

Alligators live in all 67 Florida counties, in marshes, swamps, rivers and lakes. The Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission says never to feed one, to keep your distance, to swim only in designated swimming areas during daylight, and to keep pets leashed and away from the water. Serious injuries are rare; to report a problem alligator, call 866-392-4286.

Source: myfwc.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Is there a mosquito-borne illness warning?

Yes, as of September 2026. On September 24, 2026, Pinellas County declared a State of Local Emergency for dengue fever; the Florida Department of Health's report that day counted 179 confirmed cases in Pinellas, Pasco and Hillsborough counties, 7 of them in Pinellas. The county advises light-colored long sleeves and closed-toe shoes outdoors and repellent with DEET, picaridin, IR3535 or oil of lemon eucalyptus, and notes that the mosquitoes that spread dengue can bite during the day.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How do I get emergency alerts in Hillsborough County?

Sign up for HCFL Alert, the county's official notification system, for emergency alerts such as hurricane evacuations and flood warnings, and for notice of when evacuation zones are called.

Source: hcfl.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How do I get emergency alerts in Pinellas County?

Sign up for Alert Pinellas, a free service shared by the county, its cities and the Sheriff's Office. You choose phone, text or email and can follow up to five locations, such as your hotel's.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What is an evacuation zone?

An area that may be ordered to evacuate because of storm surge. The Florida Division of Emergency Management says zones run from A to F: Zone A is usually the most vulnerable and evacuated first. Not every zone is ordered to leave in every storm, so listen for orders from local officials.

Source: floridadisaster.org (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How do I find the evacuation zone for a Tampa address?

Use Hillsborough County's Hurricane Evacuation Assessment Tool (HEAT): enter the address on the county's Find Evacuation Information page to see which of the five evacuation zones it is in. HART runs free evacuation buses to shelters.

Source: hcfl.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How do I find the evacuation zone for a Pinellas address?

Pinellas County's Know Your Zone app gives the evacuation zone for an address, plus the closest shelters and hotels. You can also call (727) 464-3800 for help looking up a zone.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I text 911?

Pinellas County says deaf and hard-of-hearing people can text 9-1-1 in an emergency. For a fire, police or medical emergency, call 9-1-1.

Source: pinellas.gov (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

What accessibility help does Visit Tampa Bay offer?

Visit Tampa Bay says Tampa Bay is an Accessibility Verified destination with Wheel the World, whose site lists accessible hotels and activities. The Unlock Tampa Bay Visitors Center hands out Hidden Disabilities Sunflower lanyards and rents EnChroma glasses for red-green color blindness (call ahead); the Tampa Museum of Art and the Florida Museum of Photographic Arts also have them.

Are there beach access mats on Clearwater Beach?

Yes, just south of Pier 60. The City of Clearwater lays 260 feet of mat from November 1 to May 14 and shortens it to 100 feet from Beach Walk during sea turtle nesting season (May 15 to October 31). The mats do not reach the water line.

Source: myclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I borrow a beach wheelchair on Clearwater Beach?

Yes, at no charge. The City of Clearwater lends manual beach wheelchairs with balloon tires at Lifeguard Tower 0 (180 S. Gulfview Blvd.) for use from 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., first come, first served, with a signed release and ID. An accompanying adult must assist, and the chairs are for the beach and shallow water only. Phone (727) 444-8580.

Source: myclearwater.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Which other Pinellas beaches lend beach wheelchairs?

Visit St. Pete/Clearwater's accessible beaches guide (updated August 19, 2025) lists beach wheelchairs, lent first come, first served, at Fort De Soto Park (five), Honeymoon Island State Park (four), Sand Key Park (two, near Boardwalk 4), Fred Howard Park (two, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.), Archibald Memorial Beach Park in Madeira Beach (one), Treasure Island (one at City Hall on weekdays, two more at the concession stand), the St. Pete Beach public access at Paradise Grill (two), Upham Beach (two) and Pass-a-Grille Beach (three, at Paradise Grille). It says the chairs at Honeymoon Island, Sand Key, Pass-a-Grille and the Treasure Island concession stand are free, and its beach FAQ lists Fort De Soto among the free ones.

How does Busch Gardens handle ride access for guests with disabilities?

Through its Ride Accessibility Program (RAP): you enroll at Guest Relations inside the park entrance, get a list of rides that fit your abilities, and use a virtual queue for select rides. Wheelchairs and ECVs can be rented inside the park to the right of Guest Relations and reserved online.

Is the Clearwater Marine Aquarium accessible?

The aquarium says it has accessible elevators and ramps throughout, but its eco-boat tours are not wheelchair accessible. With 14 days' notice it arranges sign language interpreting at no charge; phone 727-441-1790 ext. 0.

What access services does the Dalí Museum offer?

The Dalí lends courtesy wheelchairs first come, first served, offers free ASL interpretation with two weeks' notice (727-623-4706), has induction neck loops, large-print tour booklets and a sensory-friendly guide, and holds sensory-friendly events.

Do I pay to park at the beach with a disabled parking permit?

In Clearwater, vehicles with handicap tags or placards do not pay at city-owned paid or timed spaces, but the city says posted time limits and restrictions must still be followed. In Pinellas County parks, a state disabled parking placard, a disabled license plate or a Disabled Veterans plate lets you park free in any space.

Is the Florida Aquarium accessible?

The aquarium says it has wheelchair access to all habitats and exhibits and elevators to all upper levels. It rents wheelchairs ($8) and scooters ($15) daily in limited numbers, first come, first served, works with USF's Center for Autism and Related Disabilities, and holds Day of Discovery sessions for guests with sensory differences.

Is MOSI accessible?

MOSI lends wheelchairs at no cost, first come, first served, at its admissions desk (you leave a driver's license or credit card until you return it); the Holloway Digital Dome Theatre has an elevator entrance on the 4th floor. Service dogs are permitted. USF's Center for Autism and Related Disabilities helped shape its exhibits and programs. For other accommodations, call Guest Services at (813) 987-6000 at least 15 working days before your visit.

Is the St. Pete Pier accessible?

The Pier's trams carry wheelchair users and walkers, and smaller electric shuttles also take wheelchairs. The Pier says it has no wheelchair rentals at this time.

Is the Tampa Museum of Art accessible?

The museum lends wheelchairs free, first come, first served, and all galleries are reachable by elevator. The closest accessible parking is on the 4th and 5th floors of the William F. Poe Garage (802 N. Ashley Dr.). It opens early for low-sensory Art on the Spectrum visits on the last Sunday of each month.
