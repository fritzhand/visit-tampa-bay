Signature places

Things to do

The places the region's official visitor guides lead with, two from each sheet that has them.

TPBusch Gardens Tampa Bay

Tampa · Theme park · North Tampa

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

A theme park that combines roller coasters and thrill rides with a zoo of more than 200 animal species, safaris, animal encounters and live shows. Seasonal events include Howl-O-Scream and Christmas Town.

TPBayshore Boulevard

Tampa · Waterfront · Hyde Park & SoHo

Bayshore Boulevard

A waterfront boulevard along Hillsborough Bay with a 4.5-mile, 10-foot-wide sidewalk and balustrade, a bike lane and a linear park with benches, a water fountain and workout stations. Wikipedia describes the sidewalk as the second-longest continuous sidewalk in the United States.

SPThe St. Pete Pier

St. Pete · Pier · Downtown St. Petersburg

The St. Pete Pier

Pier district on Tampa Bay with seven restaurants (Pier Teak has a rooftop level), public art, the Glazer Family Playground and a splash pad, Spa Beach, a fishing deck, a weekend marketplace and the Tampa Bay Watch Discovery Center. The St. Petersburg Museum of History sits at its entrance.

SPSunshine Skyway Bridge

St. Pete · Landmark · South St. Pete & Midtown

Sunshine Skyway Bridge

Cable-stayed toll bridge carrying I-275 and US 19 across Lower Tampa Bay from St. Petersburg to Terra Ceia in Manatee County, about 4.1 miles long with a 1,200-foot main span; it opened on April 20, 1987. It replaced the original Skyway, part of which fell in 1980 when a freighter struck it.

GBClearwater Beach (public beach)

Gulf Beaches · Beach · Clearwater Beach

Clearwater Beach (public beach)

The City of Clearwater's public Gulf beach at the west end of State Road 60, with public entrances from South Gulfview Boulevard near Opal Sands to Eldorado Avenue, lifeguards year round (towers staffed daily 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.), volleyball, and the Beach Walk promenade. No alcohol, no animals except service animals, and no fishing on the public bathing beaches.

GBJohn's Pass Village & Boardwalk

Gulf Beaches · District · Madeira Beach & John's Pass

John's Pass Village & Boardwalk

Waterfront boardwalk village at John's Pass with more than 100 merchants: shops, restaurants, the local fishing fleet, dolphin-watching and shelling tours, boat rentals, parasailing and jet skis. The beach is a short walk away.

CWClearwater Marine Aquarium

Clearwater · Aquarium · Downtown Clearwater

Clearwater Marine Aquarium

Nonprofit aquarium and working marine animal hospital that rescues, rehabilitates and releases marine life; visitors see resident dolphins, sea turtles, and California sea lions and harbor seals at the new Pinniped Pier. It keeps a 'Tales of Winter the Dolphin' exhibit.

CWHoneymoon Island State Park

Clearwater · State park · Dunedin

Honeymoon Island State Park

A barrier-island state park just west of Dunedin with about four miles of Gulf beach (swimming on the main beach, a pet beach on the south end, shelling on the northern sand spit), the 2.5-mile Osprey Trail through virgin slash pine, the Rotary Centennial Nature Center, a cafe and rentals of bikes, kayaks, umbrellas and chairs. The ferry to Caladesi Island leaves from here.

DTLEGOLAND Florida Resort

Day Trips · Theme park · Lakeland, Winter Haven & Polk County

LEGOLAND Florida Resort

A 150-acre LEGO theme park in Winter Haven built for families with children 2 to 12, with more than 50 rides, shows and attractions, Miniland USA, a water park, a SEA LIFE aquarium and the preserved botanical gardens of the old Cypress Gardens, plus three themed hotels. It opened on October 15, 2011, on the Cypress Gardens grounds.

DTSiesta Beach

Day Trips · Beach · Sarasota

Siesta Beach

Sarasota County's main Siesta Key beach, where the county says the nearly all-quartz sand never gets hot; a 2016 renovation added a playground, two concession stands, more restrooms, a half-mile esplanade and pavilions, and its 950 free parking spaces are the most at any county beach. Lifeguards are on duty daily; alcohol is allowed but glass is not, and only service animals may come. The county lists U.S. News & World Report's No. 1 U.S. beach ranking for 2026.

Getting here

Getting around

TPA

Tampa International Airport (TPA)

Tampa's airport, about five miles west of downtown Tampa, has a central main terminal linked by automated shuttles to Airsides A, C, E and F. The SkyConnect train runs from the main terminal to the Economy Parking garage and the Rental Car Center, where the HART and PSTA bus stop is; Yellow Cab taxis wait outside baggage claim and charge a $40 flat rate to downtown Tampa and the cruise terminal.

Tampa · tampaairport.com (opens in a new tab)

PIE

St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport (PIE)

The airport on Roosevelt Blvd (SR 686), 4 miles north of St. Petersburg, served by Allegiant and Sun Country (domestic) and BermudAir (international), with nonstops to more than 60 cities. Rideshare pickup is in front of the terminal at Arrivals; taxis and shuttles wait in the Ground Transportation lot outside baggage claim; PSTA's Grouper runs to Clearwater Beach.

Clearwater & North Pinellas, St. Petersburg and Gulf Beaches · fly2pie.com (opens in a new tab)

SRQ

Sarasota Bradenton International Airport (SRQ)

The airport serving Sarasota and Bradenton, run by the Sarasota Manatee Airport Authority; Uber and Lyft pick up at the far west end of the ticket wing in the Ground Transportation area, taxis must be called ahead, and Sarasota County Breeze Transit and Manatee County Area Transit (MCAT) buses stop there.
