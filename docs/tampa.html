Sheet 1 · TP

Tampa

Tampa, the county seat of Hillsborough County and, per Visit Tampa Bay, Florida's third-largest city, sits on the north shore of Tampa Bay, with the Hillsborough River running through downtown. The 2.6-mile Tampa Riverwalk follows the river past downtown's museums and parks, the Channel District next door has the Florida Aquarium and the cruise terminals, the free TECO Line Streetcar runs to Ybor City's cigar-era streets, and Busch Gardens and ZooTampa are north of downtown.

Known for the Tampa Riverwalk, Ybor City and its cigar history, the Cuban sandwich, Busch Gardens Tampa Bay, cruises from Port Tampa Bay and the Buccaneers and the Lightning.

TPDowntown Tampa

Tampa · District

Downtown Tampa

Tampa's central business district, along the Hillsborough River. The 2.6-mile Tampa Riverwalk links its parks, museums and theaters, and the University of Tampa campus in the West Riverfront neighborhood holds the Henry B. Plant Museum.

the Tampa Riverwalk · Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park · Tampa Theatre · the Straz Center · Tampa Museum of Art · the Henry B. Plant Museum at the University of Tampa

Tampa · District

Channel District & Water Street

A former port warehouse district that has become downtown Tampa's most densely residential neighborhood, home to the Florida Aquarium, Sparkman Wharf and the cruise ship terminals. Water Street Tampa, a development of more than 16 city blocks, adds hotels, Benchmark International Arena and the Tampa Bay History Center.

the Florida Aquarium · Sparkman Wharf · cruise ship terminals · Benchmark International Arena · the Tampa Bay History Center · the American Victory Ship & Museum

TPHarbour Island

Tampa · Island

Harbour Island

A 177-acre island across the Garrison Channel from downtown, once a rail-served phosphate terminal and developed since 1985 with homes, offices and the Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside hotel. The Tampa Convention Center is across the Harbour Island Bridge, and the Channel District is within walking distance.

the Westin Tampa Downtown Waterside · The Pointe at Harbour Island · the Harbour Island Bridge to the Tampa Convention Center

Tampa · Island

Davis Islands

Two man-made islands at the mouth of the Hillsborough River, dredged and filled in the 1920s land boom by developer D. P. Davis, with many of the original Mediterranean-style buildings still standing. East Davis Boulevard has shops and cafes, a 1.3-mile trail runs to Peter O. Knight Airport, and Tampa General Hospital fills the northern tip.

1920s Mediterranean-style buildings · shops and cafes on East Davis Boulevard · the Davis Islands Trail · Peter O. Knight Airport and its seaplane basin · the Davis Island Yacht Club

TPYbor City

Tampa · District

Ybor City

Tampa's historic cigar-making district and a National Historic Landmark District, where converted brick cigar factories hold restaurants, cigar shops, museums and 7th Avenue's bars and music venues. The TECO Line Streetcar runs here from downtown, and free-roaming chickens have walked its streets for over a century.

cigar factories and hand-rolled cigars · the Columbia Restaurant · the Ybor City Museum State Park · nightlife on 7th Avenue · the free-roaming Ybor chickens · the TECO Line Streetcar

TPTampa Heights

Tampa · Neighborhood

Tampa Heights

Founded in the 1880s as Tampa's first suburb, Tampa Heights lies just north of downtown between I-275, the Hillsborough River and Martin Luther King Jr. Boulevard. Its riverfront has Armature Works, a restored historic building with a food hall and restaurants, and Water Works Park.

Armature Works · Water Works Park · bungalow-lined streets · coffee shops and breweries

TPHyde Park & SoHo

Tampa · Neighborhood

Hyde Park & SoHo

A historic neighborhood of homes and bungalows first built in the 1880s, next to the University of Tampa and bounded by Bayshore Boulevard and its 4.5-mile waterfront sidewalk. It includes Hyde Park Village, a shopping district of local shops and restaurants, and SoHo, the bar and restaurant strip on South Howard Avenue.

Bayshore Boulevard · Hyde Park Village · SoHo bars and restaurants on South Howard Avenue · historic homes and bungalows

TPWest Tampa

Tampa · Neighborhood

West Tampa

Founded in 1892 as a cigar-making town and settled mostly by Cuban immigrants, West Tampa was its own city until Tampa annexed it in 1925; it lies west of the Hillsborough River about a mile from downtown. This area of the guide also takes in Raymond James Stadium, home of the Buccaneers, and George M. Steinbrenner Field, the Yankees' spring training ballpark, which face each other across Dale Mabry Highway.

cigar-era history · Cuban food · Raymond James Stadium and its pirate ship · George M. Steinbrenner Field · the 1914 West Tampa Free Public Library

TPSeminole Heights

Tampa · Neighborhood

Seminole Heights

A streetcar suburb begun in 1911 about three miles north of downtown, known for its early 20th-century Craftsman bungalows and a local and national historic district. Restaurants, brewpubs and independent shops have opened here in recent years, and its parks give it the city's longest stretch of Hillsborough River parkland.

Craftsman bungalows · restaurants and brewpubs · riverfront parks on the Hillsborough River · the two-headed alligator statue on Nebraska Avenue

TPNorth Tampa

Tampa · District

North Tampa

The northern part of the city and its suburbs, with Busch Gardens Tampa Bay and its sister water park Adventure Island, ZooTampa at Lowry Park by the Hillsborough River, and MOSI, which has a pedestrian bridge to the University of South Florida. Includes the historic Sulphur Springs neighborhood, New Tampa and Carrollwood.

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay · Adventure Island · ZooTampa at Lowry Park · MOSI and its planetarium · the University of South Florida

TPWestshore & Airport

Tampa · District

Westshore & Airport

A principal business district of Tampa that takes in Tampa International Airport, with office towers, many hotels and two major malls, International Plaza and WestShore Plaza. Includes Rocky Point, with hotels and restaurants at the Tampa end of the Courtney Campbell Causeway to Clearwater, and Town 'n' Country.

Tampa International Airport · International Plaza · WestShore Plaza · airport-area hotels · Rocky Point on the Courtney Campbell Causeway

TPSouth Tampa

Tampa · District

South Tampa

The south end of the city below Hyde Park, reached along Bayshore Boulevard, whose waterfront path runs to Gandy Boulevard. Ballast Point Park looks across Hillsborough Bay to downtown, Palma Ceia has brick streets named for streets in Havana, and Picnic Island Park in Port Tampa, at the city's southwestern tip, has a sand beach, a dog beach and a kayak launch nearby.

Ballast Point Park · Picnic Island Park and its beach · Palma Ceia's brick streets · the Bayshore Boulevard greenway

TPEast Tampa & the Fairgrounds

Tampa · District

East Tampa & the Fairgrounds

The city's East Tampa neighborhoods, north of I-4 and east of I-275, and the Orient Road and US 301 area beyond them. Visitors come here for the Seminole Hard Rock Hotel & Casino and the Florida State Fairgrounds, home of the Florida State Fair, the MIDFLORIDA Credit Union Amphitheatre and the Cracker Country living history museum.

Signature places

The places the region's official visitor guides lead with. The number is the buoy on the chart above.

TPBusch Gardens Tampa Bay

Tampa · Theme park · North Tampa

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

A theme park that combines roller coasters and thrill rides with a zoo of more than 200 animal species, safaris, animal encounters and live shows. Seasonal events include Howl-O-Scream and Christmas Town.

2
TPZooTampa at Lowry Park

Tampa · Zoo · North Tampa

ZooTampa at Lowry Park

A zoo in Lowry Park on West Sligh Avenue, open daily. Wikipedia describes it as a 63-acre zoo with more than 1,300 animals; its Florida section includes West Indian manatees.

13
TPThe Florida Aquarium

Tampa · Aquarium · Channel District & Water Street

The Florida Aquarium

A nonprofit aquarium on the Channel District waterfront, accredited by the Association of Zoos & Aquariums. Besides the galleries it runs a Wild Dolphin Cruise on a 72-foot catamaran and paid encounters such as penguin meetings and stingray feeding.

7
TPHenry B. Plant Museum

Tampa · Museum · Downtown Tampa

Henry B. Plant Museum

A museum in the former Tampa Bay Hotel, Henry B. Plant's winter resort of 1891–1932, a building it shares with the University of Tampa. Its rooms show the furnishings used by the hotel's first guests.

4
TPYbor City Museum State Park

Tampa · Museum · Ybor City

Ybor City Museum State Park

A state museum in the former Ferlita Bakery, with self-guided exhibits, audio and a video on the founding of Ybor City, the cigar industry and the immigrant groups who worked in it. The grounds include an ornamental garden and a restored cigar worker's house, La Casita, with guided tours on request.

14
TPBayshore Boulevard

Tampa · Waterfront · Hyde Park & SoHo

Bayshore Boulevard

A waterfront boulevard along Hillsborough Bay with a 4.5-mile, 10-foot-wide sidewalk and balustrade, a bike lane and a linear park with benches, a water fountain and workout stations. Wikipedia describes the sidewalk as the second-longest continuous sidewalk in the United States.

8
TPTampa Riverwalk

Tampa · Waterfront · Downtown Tampa

Tampa Riverwalk

A 2.6-mile waterfront path along the Hillsborough River from Beneficial Drive at Channelside Drive to just past Armature Works, linking downtown parks, museums, hotels and restaurants. Its Historical Monument Trail has busts and monuments to 30 honorees from Tampa's history.

5
TPYbor City Historic District

Tampa · Historic district · Ybor City

Ybor City Historic District

A National Historic Landmark district northeast of downtown Tampa, founded in 1886 as a planned cigar-making town. It keeps cigar factories, immigrant social-club buildings, worker houses and the 7th Avenue commercial strip.

9
TPDavid A. Straz Jr. Center for the Performing Arts

Tampa · Performing arts · Downtown Tampa

David A. Straz Jr. Center for the Performing Arts

Downtown Tampa's performing arts center on the Hillsborough River, with five theaters (the largest, Carol Morsani Hall, seats 2,610) for touring Broadway shows, opera, dance, concerts, comedy and theater. It opened in 1987 as the Tampa Bay Performing Arts Center and is home to Opera Tampa, Jobsite Theater and the Patel Conservatory.

10

Tampa · Performing arts · Downtown Tampa

Tampa Theatre

A 1926 movie palace on Franklin Street that now shows new and classic films and hosts concerts, educational programs and tours. Its listing says it holds more than 600 events a year.

11
TPBenchmark International Arena

Tampa · Arena or stadium · Channel District & Water Street

Benchmark International Arena

Indoor arena in Tampa's Channel District and the home of the Tampa Bay Lightning (NHL); it also books arena concerts and touring shows. It opened in 1996 as the Ice Palace and was called Amalie Arena from 2014 until it was renamed in August 2025.

3
TPRaymond James Stadium

Tampa · Arena or stadium · West Tampa

Raymond James Stadium

Open-air stadium on North Dale Mabry Highway, home of the Tampa Bay Buccaneers and USF Bulls football, and host of the ReliaQuest Bowl, the Gasparilla Bowl, Monster Jam and stadium concerts. It is managed by the Tampa Sports Authority and has a replica pirate ship in the north end zone.

12
TPArmature Works

Tampa · Food hall · Tampa Heights

Armature Works

A restored 1910 Tampa Electric streetcar warehouse on the Hillsborough River that now holds the Heights Public Market food hall, restaurants, a rooftop event space and event halls. The Riverwalk ends just past it, and it has public boat docks and a Pirate Water Taxi stop.

6

Tampa · Restaurant · Ybor City

Columbia Restaurant

The Columbia began as a corner saloon opened in 1903 and became a restaurant in 1905; it now fills a city block of Ybor City with 15 dining rooms and seating for up to 1,700. It serves Spanish-Cuban food from family recipes and stages flamenco shows, and it calls itself Florida's oldest restaurant.

15
TPBern's Steak House

Tampa · Restaurant · Hyde Park & SoHo

Bern's Steak House

A steakhouse on South Howard Avenue open since 1956, known for prime steaks dry-aged in house, a wine collection of more than half a million bottles and the Harry Waugh Dessert Room. The 2026 MICHELIN Guide lists it as a recommended restaurant.

TPBen T. Davis Beach

Tampa · Beach · Westshore & Airport

Ben T. Davis Beach

A City of Tampa bay beach on the Courtney Campbell Causeway; the state health department tests its water under the Florida Healthy Beaches Program.

Sunrise to sunset. · Metered parking, $2.50 per hour.

TPCypress Point Park

Tampa · Beach · Westshore & Airport

Cypress Point Park

A City of Tampa park on Tampa Bay with a bay beach, paved and unpaved shaded trails, a disc golf course, a sand volleyball court and a self-service paddleboard rental station; planes landing at Tampa International pass overhead.

Sunrise to sunset. · Free parking weekdays until noon; otherwise metered, $1 the first hour and $2 each hour after.

TPDavis Islands Beach and Dog Beach

Tampa · Beach · Davis Islands

Davis Islands Beach and Dog Beach

One of four City of Tampa beaches, at the Davis Islands Seaplane Basin (864 Severn Ave.); the city's Davis Islands Dog Park & Dog Beach is nearby at 1002 Severn St. The state health department tests the water under the Florida Healthy Beaches Program.

Sunrise to sunset. · Price not listed

TPPicnic Island Park

Tampa · Beach · South Tampa

Picnic Island Park

A City of Tampa park in far South Tampa with a bay beach, a separate gated dog beach south of the fishing pier, a boat ramp, a disc golf course, a sand volleyball court and a playground; a mangrove-tunnel kayak launch is across the street.

Sunrise to sunset; boat ramp open 24 hours. · Price not listed

TPCurtis Hixon Waterfront Park

Tampa · Park · Downtown Tampa

Curtis Hixon Waterfront Park

Downtown Tampa waterfront park whose great lawn, with sloped and terraced edges, hosts festivals and concerts. It also has two splash pads, a playground, a fenced dog park, a boat dock and a Pirate Water Taxi stop.

Hours not listed · Price not listed

TPCotanchobee Fort Brooke Park

Tampa · Park · Channel District & Water Street

Cotanchobee Fort Brooke Park

A 4.5-acre waterfront park on the Garrison Channel that takes part of its name from Fort Brooke, the Army post of 1824 around which Tampa grew. It has a memorial to the Seminole Indian Wars, a cenotaph by Native American artist Bob Haozous, a playground and splash pad, and the Riverwalk's end point.

7 AM–10 PM daily. · Price not listed

Getting around

Getting around

Arriving

  • Tampa International Airport TPAAirportTampa's airport, about five miles west of downtown Tampa, has a central main terminal linked by automated shuttles to Airsides A, C, E and F. The SkyConnect train runs from the main terminal to the Economy Parking garage and the Rental Car Center, where the HART and PSTA bus stop is; Yellow Cab taxis wait outside baggage claim and charge a $40 flat rate to downtown Tampa and the cruise terminal.
  • Port Tampa Bay cruise terminalsCruise portTampa homeports eight ships from five cruise lines (Carnival, Royal Caribbean, Celebrity, Norwegian and Margaritaville at Sea) at three terminals along Channelside Drive in the Channel District; the port's garage is across the street from Terminal 3. The terminals have no luggage storage for early arrivals; the port says the Florida Aquarium next to Terminal 2 stores luggage free with a discounted admission ticket.
  • Amtrak at Tampa Union Station Station code TPARailTampa's Amtrak station, an Italian Renaissance-style building that opened in May 1912, is served by the Floridian, which Amtrak describes as a temporary combination of the Capitol Limited and Silver Star running daily between Chicago and Miami via Washington, DC.
  • Greyhound and FlixBus (Tampa Bus Station)Intercity busGreyhound and FlixBus intercity buses use the Tampa Bus Station at HART's Marion Transit Center downtown, with a second stop on the USF campus (Holly Drive at Lot 3D, the Fine Arts Bull Runner stop); both sites list 28 cities reachable directly from Tampa, among them Orlando, Miami, Tallahassee, Atlanta, Fort Myers and Jacksonville.
  • RedCoach (Tampa stop at USF)Intercity busRedCoach intercity buses stop on the USF campus (3801 USF Holly Drive) and run from Tampa to Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Fort Myers, Naples, Gainesville, Ocala and Tallahassee (FSU).

Transit, trolleys and boats

  • TECO Line StreetcarStreetcar · Free: all rides are fare free.A free electric heritage streetcar on a 2.7-mile line with 11 covered, wheelchair-accessible stations linking downtown Tampa, the Channel District and Ybor City; an end-to-end trip takes about 25 minutes. Pull the overhead cord to request your stop; bikes are not allowed on board.
  • Pirate Water TaxiWater taxiA hop-on, hop-off water taxi on the Hillsborough River and the Channel District waterfront with 10 numbered stops from the Florida Aquarium to Armature Works; an all-day pass covers unlimited rides. Boats are covered and wheelchair accessible, though not every dock is; bikes and luggage are not allowed on board, and service stops for lightning, heavy rain and high winds.
  • Tampa Bay Ferry (St. Petersburg-Tampa)FerryThere is no passenger ferry across the bay between downtown St. Petersburg and Tampa right now: PSTA is preparing a new Tampa Bay Ferry service to replace the former Cross-Bay Ferry, with two vessels (Bay Breeze and Clipper) being refurbished in Tarpon Springs and a permanent dock planned in downtown St. Petersburg.
  • 727 Airport Express (downtown St. Petersburg to Tampa International Airport)BusA new PSTA premium express bus between nine stops in downtown St. Petersburg and Tampa International Airport, running over the Howard Frankland Bridge express lanes on I-275. PSTA says it will run daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. on hybrid buses with luggage racks.
  • HART busesBusHillsborough County's bus system, with local routes, limited-express commuter routes and HARTFlex, meeting at the Marion Transit Center downtown. Routes 10 (Cypress St.) and 30 (Kennedy Blvd./Town 'N Country) link downtown Tampa with the Tampa International Airport Rental Car Center, reached from the terminal by the SkyConnect train.
  • PSTA Route 100 Express (St. Petersburg to Tampa)Bus · Regular PSTA fare ($2.25).A weekday express bus between the St. Pete Pier area (2nd Ave NE and Beach Drive) and the Marion Transit Center in downtown Tampa, stopping at Gateway Mall and Britton Plaza.
  • PSTA Route 300 Express (Largo to Tampa Airport and downtown Tampa)Bus · Regular PSTA fare ($2.25).A weekday limited-stop express from the Ulmerton Park and Ride in Largo via the Tampa International Airport Rental Car Center to the Marion Transit Center in downtown Tampa.
  • DASH (Downtown Area Shared Hubs)MicrotransitAn app-hailed, hub-to-hub ride service in downtown Tampa using all-electric vehicles, with more than twenty virtual hubs; each ride costs $2 per person. Wheelchair-accessible rides are provided by partner Wheelchair Transport Service (813-540-3333).
