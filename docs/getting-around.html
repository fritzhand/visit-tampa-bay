Grouper Airport Express (PIE to Clearwater Beach)

PSTA

A shared, bookable shuttle between St. Pete-Clearwater International Airport (PIE) and any address on Clearwater Beach, including the Sheraton and Marriott Sand Key resorts; book through grouper.psta.net or the Grouper app. At PIE the pickup is through the west baggage claim exit doors; drivers help with luggage and the service is wheelchair accessible.

Fare
One-way: $8 single rider (seniors 65+ $4); $20 total for groups of 3-4; $30 total for groups of 5-8. The airport's own page lists higher fares ($10 single, $30 for four, $40 for five to eight).
Hours
Every day and time the airport has inbound or outbound flights; book on demand or up to 2 months ahead.

What is DASH in downtown Tampa?

DASH (Downtown Area Shared Hubs) is an app-hailed ride service run by the Tampa Downtown Partnership between more than twenty virtual hubs downtown, in all-electric vehicles, for $2 per person. It runs Monday to Saturday 7 AM-11 PM and Sunday 12 PM-9 PM.

Source: tampasdowntown.com (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

How much does the Pirate Water Taxi cost, and where do I board?

An all-day hop-on, hop-off pass costs $34 for adults and $24 for children 2-12 (prices can change). You can board at any of its 10 stops; the ticket booth is at Stop 7, on the Riverwalk between the Tampa Convention Center and the Marriott Water Street. The boats are cashless, and no reservation is needed.

Source: piratewatertaxi.com (opens in a new tab) (2 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

When does the Pirate Water Taxi run?

Monday to Thursday 11:30 AM-6:30 PM and Friday to Sunday 11:30 AM-9:30 PM. Service stops for lightning, heavy rain and high winds, and the operator says tides can affect the route and stops.

Source: piratewatertaxi.com (opens in a new tab) (3 pages)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Can I bring a bike or my dog on the streetcar?

Bikes are not allowed on the TECO Line Streetcar. Service dogs may ride; other dogs must be in small carriers. Folded strollers are allowed, and every station has a ramp and every car is wheelchair accessible.

Source: tecolinestreetcar.org (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Is the Tampa streetcar being extended?

The City of Tampa has studied extending and modernizing the streetcar: its preferred plan adds a 1.3-mile line from the current western end through downtown to Tampa Heights and replaces the replica cars with modern streetcars. The city's project page gives no construction or opening date.

Source: tampa.gov (opens in a new tab)Checked Sep 27, 2026

Do I need a SunPass to drive around Tampa Bay?

No, but it is cheaper. The Tampa-area toll roads on Florida's Turnpike list (Veterans Expressway, Suncoast Parkway, I-4 Connector, Selmon Expressway and the Sunshine Skyway Bridge) do not accept cash. With SunPass, or an accepted transponder such as E-ZPass, E-PASS or Peach Pass, you pay the lowest toll rate; without one, Toll-By-Plate photographs the plate and mails the owner an invoice with a $2.50 administrative fee. Florida's Turnpike says SunPass saves an average of 25%.

How do tolls work in a rental car?

Most rental companies bill tolls through their own programs, each with its own fees, so check your agreement. If you have a SunPass PRO or other portable transponder, add the rental car's plate and rental dates to your SunPass account and remove the transponder when you return the car. Toll-By-Plate is not available for rental cars.
