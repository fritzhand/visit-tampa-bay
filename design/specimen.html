The plate

Sheet 6 · DT · Day TripsA record with no cleared imageLandmark

When a record has no rights-cleared image, its card and page show a plate: a cigar-label panel in the sheet's tint with concave corners, a double rule, a medallion holding the record's chart symbol, the name in Bodoni italic and the sheet code.

The plate is typography, not a picture: it is never a stock photo, a screenshot of a map or an image from an operator's website.

9Rules of use

Do and don't

Do

  • Show a sheet as code + shape + ink, with its name in text nearby.
  • Use gold for ornament and the signature seal; use magenta for links, focus, courses and the live flare.
  • Print unknowns as unknowns: “Time not listed”, with the dashed-circle mark.
  • Keep one label frame per page (the page head) and one ribbon per frame.

Don't

  • Draw soundings, depth contours, buoys or coordinates that read as navigation data.
  • Set text in gold on cream, or any text under 12px.
  • Use a paper, parchment or wood texture image. Every ornament is SVG or CSS on tokens.
  • Use a sheet ink for anything but that sheet's identity.
